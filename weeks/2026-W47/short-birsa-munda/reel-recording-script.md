## Script

A young man of just 24 shook the British Empire, and the people called him Dharti Aaba, the Father of the Earth. Today is his birthday!

ఎవరు ఆయన?

Let's decode.

Birsa Munda was born on November 15, 1875, at Ulihatu, in today's Jharkhand. Since 2021, India marks his birthday as Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, Tribal Pride Day, and this year is his 151st birth anniversary.

Why did he rise? Outsiders, moneylenders and landlords were taking Munda land, and people were forced into unpaid labour. So in 1899 Birsa led the Ulgulan, the Great Tumult, demanding that the land and the forest belong to the Munda people.

He died in Ranchi jail in June 1900, at about 24. But his struggle won: in 1908 the British passed the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act, which restricts the transfer of tribal land to outsiders. And Jharkhand itself was formed on his birthday, November 15, 2000.

చాలా proud moment!

Prelims trap: do not mix the tribal revolts. The Ulgulan of 1899 was led by Birsa Munda; the Santhal Hul of 1855 was led by Sidhu and Kanhu; and the Kol uprising came earlier, in 1831.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

His fight for land and forest lives on in today's laws, like the panchayat extension law for Scheduled Areas and the Forest Rights Act. Mains question: do these laws give tribal communities real control over their land?
