## Script

Right now, Cyclone Akvan is crossing our coast, and Srikakulam and Vizianagaram are on red alert.

అందరూ జాగ్రత్తగా ఉండండి!

Here are six points for your exam.

One, where: it is crossing the north Andhra and south Odisha coast, near Gopalpur in Odisha's Ganjam district.

Two, the name: Akvan was given by Iran, and it comes from a demon in the Persian epic, the Shahnameh.

Three, who names cyclones: 13 countries around the North Indian Ocean gave 169 names in 2020, used strictly in order, and India's weather office in New Delhi assigns them. So the name has nothing to do with where the storm hits.

Four, how strong: the weather office grades storms by wind speed. A cyclonic storm starts at 62 kilometres an hour, a severe one at 89, and a very severe one at 118.

Five, why the Bay of Bengal: its warmer waters, and storms drifting in from the Pacific, make it far more cyclone-prone than the Arabian Sea.

Six, the Prelims trap: Yanam sits on the Andhra coast, but it is part of Puducherry, not Andhra Pradesh.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: early warnings now save lives, but how do we also save crops and homes?

మీ అభిప్రాయం comments లో రాయండి!
