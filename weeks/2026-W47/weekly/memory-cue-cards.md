**Video 1 · ICJ and Myanmar:**
1. **19 Nov**, the world court (ICJ) rules **9–6**: Myanmar committed **genocide** against the Rohingya (2017, Rakhine, ~10,000 killed).
2. It's the **first time** a country itself has been found guilty. Myanmar must pay compensation and report back in **2 years**.
3. **The Gambia** filed the case in **2019** for the OIC (57 countries), under the **Genocide Convention, 1948**.
4. India: ratified the convention in **1959**; hosts Rohingya, but is **not** in the 1951 Refugee Convention.
5. **Trap:** ICJ = countries · ICC = individuals (India is not a member).

**Video 2 · Shenzhen:**
1. **19 Nov**: first-ever **Trump–Xi–Putin** summit, in Shenzhen (at the APEC meeting).
2. They promised **talks on nuclear risks**, but with no deadline.
3. **New START** (the US–Russia treaty) lapsed in **Feb 2026**, so nothing now caps their arsenals.
4. India needs all three (US for technology, Russia for defence, China as neighbour), so **strategic autonomy** matters.
5. **Trap:** the NPT has **5** nuclear powers (US, Russia, UK, France, China); India is in neither the NPT nor the CTBT; India's policy is **no first use**.

**Video 3 · Quad in Manila:**
1. **17 Nov**: first leaders' summit in 2+ years.
2. **AI Accelerator, $85 mn+**; a **business forum in Mumbai in 2027**; cyber, maritime surveillance and critical minerals.
3. The PM also attended the **23rd ASEAN–India Summit** and the **21st East Asia Summit** (Act East).
4. **Trap:** India is **in** the Quad and the EAS, a **partner** of ASEAN (11 members), and **not in** APEC.

**Video 4 · Death penalty:**
1. UN General Assembly, **13 Nov**: a death-penalty pause passed **133–33–16**. India voted **against** (sovereignty, no consensus).
2. India's law: **Article 21**; **Bachan Singh (1980)** "rarest of rare"; pardon powers under **Article 72** (President) and **Article 161** (Governor).
3. **Trap:** only the **President** can pardon a court-martial sentence.

**Video 5 · Labour Codes:**
1. In force since **21 Nov 2025**: **4 codes replace 29 laws** (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Safety).
2. A floor wage for all; **gig workers** in law for the first time; fixed-term jobs.
3. **Trap:** labour is in the **Concurrent List**.

**Video 8 · Cyclone Akvan:**

2. Name given by **Iran** (from the Shahnameh). **13 countries**, **169 names**, used **in order**.
3. Strength scale: cyclonic storm **62**, severe **89**, very severe **118** km/h.
4. **Trap:** **Yanam is Puducherry**.
