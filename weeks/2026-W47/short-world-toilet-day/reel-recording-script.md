## Script

Did you know there is a United Nations day just for toilets? It is no joke: 3.4 billion people still do not have a safe one.

ఇది చిన్న విషయం కాదు!

Six points, one minute.

One, the date: November 19th, every year.

Two, the founder: Jack Sim of Singapore, known as Mister Toilet, started it in 2001, and the United Nations made it official in 2013. Easy trick: 19 · 01 · 13.

Three, this year's theme: safe toilets, equal futures, because a safe toilet keeps a girl in school.

Four, the global goal: Sustainable Development Goal 6.2, toilets for all and zero open defecation by 2030.

Five, India's answer: Swachh Bharat, launched on Gandhi Jayanti in 2014, built over 10 crore village toilets, and rural India became open-defecation free by 2019.

Six, the Prelims trap: village toilets come under Jal Shakti, but city toilets come under Housing and Urban Affairs.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: we built the toilets, but are we really using them?

మీ అభిప్రాయం comments లో రాయండి!
