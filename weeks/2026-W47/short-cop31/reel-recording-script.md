## Script

The world's biggest climate meeting is happening in Türkiye, but the person running the talks is from Australia! 

ఇది ఎలా?

 Let's decode.

This is the 31st Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, the treaty signed at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992. It opened in Antalya, on Türkiye's Mediterranean coast, on November ninth.

Türkiye and Australia both wanted to host it. The compromise: Türkiye hosts and holds the presidency, while Australia's climate minister, Chris Bowen, is the President of Negotiations, leading the draft texts. Two captains, one match!

India's big demand is money. At Baku in 2024, rich countries promised at least 300 billion dollars a year by 2035. India has called that too little, and here it wants a formal work programme on climate finance.

Prelims trap: the Paris Agreement came at the 21st conference, in 2015; Baku was the 29th, in 2024; Belém in Brazil was the 30th, in 2025; and Antalya is the 31st. 

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: is 300 billion dollars a year a fair deal for developing countries like India?
