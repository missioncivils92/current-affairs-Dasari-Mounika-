## Script

From November 20th, New Delhi hosts something the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation has never held before: a dialogue of civilisations! 

Pakistan ని కూడా పిలిచారు!

 Let's decode.

Prime Minister Modi proposed this Civilisational Dialogue Forum at the Tianjin summit in September 2025. It runs from November 20th to 22nd, organised by the Ministries of Culture and External Affairs, with sessions on philosophy, art and architecture, languages, and living traditions.

The organisation was born in 2001, out of the Shanghai Five of 1996. Its secretariat is in Beijing, and its regional anti-terrorism structure is in Tashkent.

Today it has 10 members: China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan; then India and Pakistan from 2017, Iran from 2023, and Belarus from 2024.

Prelims trap: Afghanistan and Mongolia are not members, and Turkmenistan was never part of it. And here is a twist: since the Bishkek summit in September, Pakistan chairs the organisation for 2026–27. 

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: can culture build trust in a grouping where security talks keep getting stuck?
