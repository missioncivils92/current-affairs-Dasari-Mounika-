## Script

This week, Manila is the busiest city in Asia: the Quad leaders meet there, alongside the summit of Southeast Asian nations, and right after that, leaders fly to Shenzhen for one more summit. 

ఈ మూడు groups లో India ఎక్కడ ఉంది?

 Let's decode.

The Quad, the Quadrilateral Security Dialogue, has four members: India, the United States, Japan and Australia. It grew out of the tsunami relief of 2004, met first in 2007, and was revived in 2017, on the sidelines of a summit in Manila itself. This will be its first leaders' summit in more than two years.

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations now has 11 members, after Timor-Leste joined in October 2025, and the Philippines chairs it this year. India is not a member, but a Comprehensive Strategic Partner, and our Act East Policy is built around it.

Now the key difference. India is a member of the East Asia Summit, with 18 members, which meets alongside the Southeast Asian summit. But India is not a member of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum, which meets in Shenzhen, China, on November 18th and 19th. That forum has 21 member economies, and India has been knocking on its door for years.

Prelims trap: the Quad, yes; the East Asia Summit, yes; the Asia-Pacific forum, no; and the Southeast Asian bloc, only a partner. And remember, the Quad has no secretariat and no treaty: it is a dialogue, not a military alliance. 

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: can India stay at the centre of the Indo-Pacific while staying outside its biggest trade forum?
