## Script

One country, one tiny island, one citizens' campaign, one energy giant and one Indian company: these are the five winners of the Earthshot Prize 2026.

వాళ్ళు ఏం చేశారు?

For each one: who, what they did, and how it helps. Let's decode.

First, Bhutan won Protect and Restore Nature, and it is the first country ever to win this prize. Its Restore Bhutan plan will revive 50,000 hectares of damaged Himalayan land, saving nearly 2 million tonnes of carbon dioxide and protecting water for about 1.6 billion people downstream, including us in India.

Second, our own Mahindra Last Mile Mobility won Clean Our Air. It made electric autos cheap to buy and to run for ordinary drivers, and it has put more than 4 lakh electric vehicles on Indian roads, cutting smoke in our crowded cities.

Third, Niue won Revive Our Oceans. This Pacific island has only about 1,700 people, yet its Ocean Wide Trust, with a new way of investing in conservation, protects an ocean area larger than the United Kingdom.

Fourth, PFAS-Free France won Build a Waste-Free World. PFAS are the forever chemicals found in non-stick pans, waterproof clothes and food packaging, and they barely break down in nature or in our bodies. Backed by more than 1.4 lakh citizens, this campaign helped France pass a 2025 law to phase out many of them.

Fifth, Denmark's Ørsted won Fix Our Climate. It was once an oil, gas and coal company; it quit oil and gas and became an offshore-wind giant, proving that even an energy company can change.

Prelims trap, from those forever chemicals: some of them are already restricted under the Stockholm Convention on persistent organic pollutants, which India has joined. Do not confuse it with the Rotterdam Convention, on consent before trading hazardous chemicals, or the Basel Convention, on hazardous waste crossing borders.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question: can India's coal-heavy power companies make a shift like Ørsted?
