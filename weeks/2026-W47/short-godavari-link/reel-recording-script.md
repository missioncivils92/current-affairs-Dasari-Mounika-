## Script

Andhra Pradesh wants to carry Godavari floodwater all the way to Rayalaseema, and Telangana is saying no. 

ఈ గొడవ ఏంటి?

 Let's decode.

The plan, first called the Polavaram–Banakacherla link and now reported as the Polavaram–Nallamala Sagar link, would lift about 200 thousand million cubic feet (200 tmc) of Godavari floodwater from Polavaram into the Krishna basin, and on to Rayalaseema. Andhra Pradesh says it uses only surplus floodwater that would otherwise flow into the sea.

Telangana objects. It says the plan breaks the Godavari tribunal award and the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act of 2014, and needs the consent of the other basin states. Remember, Polavaram itself is a national project under that Act.

So where does it stand? The environment ministry's Expert Appraisal Committee returned the proposal, the Central Water Commission has asked Andhra Pradesh for decades of flood data, and the Jal Shakti Ministry says no final decision has been taken.

Prelims trap: the Godavari tribunal gave its award in 1980, and the same judge, Justice Bachawat, also headed the first Krishna tribunal. And under Article 262, Parliament can even bar the courts from hearing such water disputes. 

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains question for Group-1: how can the two Telugu states share Godavari's surplus water without starting a new dispute?
