## 1. What is new this week (format)

- **Data + diagram on every deep topic:**
  - **Data:** bar charts at final values, a share donut and a trade-balance diagram.
  - **Diagrams:** flowcharts (inflation targeting, the women's-reservation chain, the battery loop), a ladder (the Graded Response Action Plan), a river chain (Siang), a timeline (Ninth Schedule), tables (Cabinet, exercises, days), cause maps and two-sided dilemmas.
- **Deep analysis pattern:** data → mechanism → cause and effect (and what it means for India) → trap → MCQ.
- **Kept from W45:**
  - an MCQ after every topic;
  - one Mains question;
  - voice only;
  - no short forms;
  - Telugu on the key lines.
- **New creative device:** the **"balance diagram"** for trade (goods deficit + services surplus = overall deficit). It is reusable for the current account and the budget.
