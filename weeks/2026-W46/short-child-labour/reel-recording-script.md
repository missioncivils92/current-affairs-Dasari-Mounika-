## Script

A child rolling beedis at home: is that legal in India?

ఈ case 17 సంవత్సరాలు Supreme Court లో ఉంది.

Let's decode.

In 2009, a petition challenged a clause of the child labour law that allowed children to work in beedi rolling. Before the Court could decide, Parliament changed the law in 2016.

Now the Supreme Court has closed the case, but with a direction: the Centre must set up a special cell to keep monitoring how the child labour law is enforced, and pass instructions to every state through its Chief Secretary.

So what does the law say today? No child below 14 can be employed in any occupation, except helping the family after school hours or working as a child artist; and adolescents between 14 and 18 cannot work in hazardous jobs.

Prelims trap: Article 24 of the Constitution bans children below 14 only in factories, mines and hazardous work; the complete ban on all occupations comes from the 2016 amendment to the Act, not from the Constitution.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

And with Children's Day coming on November 14, here is the Mains question: why do laws alone fail to end child labour?
