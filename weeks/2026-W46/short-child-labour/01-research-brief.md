# Research Brief — 2026-W46 Short: Supreme Court special cell on child labour (order of 29 Oct, reported 4 Nov 2026)

**Why chosen:** a fresh topic (owner, 9 Nov). It ties in with Children's Day (14 Nov).
**EYS 8 (P3·M2·I2·D1·S0):**
- **P:** Article 24; the 1986 Act and its 2016 amendment (under-14 and 14–18 rules); Article 21A.
- **M:** child labour, a recurring Mains theme.
- **I:** an Indian court and an Indian law.
- **D:** the special cell is now being set up.

| Fact | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| SC (Justices SVN Bhatti and NV Anjaria), order dated 29 Oct 2026, reported 4 Nov: disposed of a 2009 writ petition against the proviso to s.3 of the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 (beedi rolling); the 2016 amendment had largely addressed it; directed a special cell to monitor enforcement, with instructions through state Chief Secretaries | LiveLaw; Outlook; The Week; LawChakra | Verified. The cell's ministry is reported as Women and Child Development by one source, so on air it is just "the Centre" |
| 2016 amendment: no employment of children below 14 in any occupation or process, except helping the family or family enterprise (non-hazardous) after school hours, and as an artist in audio-visual entertainment; adolescents 14–18 barred from hazardous occupations | Act (standard) | Verified (standard) |
| Art 24: no child below 14 in any factory, mine or other hazardous employment | Constitution | Verified |
