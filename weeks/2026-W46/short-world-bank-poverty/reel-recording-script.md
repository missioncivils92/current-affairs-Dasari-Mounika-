## Script

Nearly 50 crore Indians, about half a billion people, have crossed a World Bank poverty line in just 12 years.

ఇది ఎలా సాధ్యం అయ్యింది?

Let's decode.

The World Bank's October Poverty and Equity Brief says extreme poverty in India, measured at 3 dollars a day, fell from 27.1 per cent in 2011 to just 2.6 per cent in 2023.

But what is this 3 dollars? In 2025 the World Bank raised its international poverty line from 2 dollars 15 to 3 dollars a day, measured in purchasing power parity, which adjusts for what money can actually buy in each country.

And at the higher line for lower-middle-income countries, 4 dollars 20 a day, India's poverty fell from 57.7 to 16.1 per cent.

అంటే, extreme poverty దాదాపు పోయింది, but many are still just above the line.

Prelims trap: the World Bank measures poverty by income and spending; the Multidimensional Poverty Index, which counts health, education and living standards, is published globally by the United Nations Development Programme and Oxford, and in India by NITI Aayog.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Critics say part of this fall comes from new data and methods. Mains question: does crossing a poverty line really mean escaping poverty?
