# Research Brief — 2026-W46 Short: cancer medicines cheaper from 9 Nov 2026

**Release:** Mon 9 Nov 2026 · YouTube Short (9:16) · about 1:20 · on camera (decision 41) · English with Telugu key lines (decision 42).
