## Script

What if the same cancer medicine costs up to 70 per cent less, starting today?

ఇది నిజం!

Let's decode.

From today, November 9, the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority has capped the trade margin on 137 anti-cancer medicines at 30 per cent.

But what is a trade margin? It is the cut taken by distributors and chemists between the company's price and the price you pay. On these medicines it averaged about 170 per cent, and in some cases even 700.

ఇప్పుడు అది 30 per cent కి limit.

But which law allows this? Paragraph 19 of the Drugs Prices Control Order of 2013 lets the government step in on prices in extraordinary situations, in public interest, even for medicines outside the essential medicines list.

Prelims trap: the pricing authority does not work under the Health Ministry; it works under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, in the Chemicals and Fertilizers Ministry.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

And this is not the first time: a 2019 pilot capped margins on 42 cancer drugs, and the prices of over 500 brands fell, some by up to 90 per cent.

Mains angle: Article 47 asks the state to improve public health, so is price control the answer, or will it hurt drug research?
