## Script

Agnipath scheme scrapped అయ్యిందా?

కాదు!

But this week, something very big changed for every Agniveer. Let's decode.

Agnipath began in June 2022 for all three services, the Army, the Navy and the Air Force: young people between 17 and a half and 21 join as Agniveers for four years, and only about one in four were to stay on.

Now the Union Cabinet has changed it: about three in four Agniveers will get two more years of service, so up to six years, with higher pay, a bigger Seva Nidhi exit package, and a pathway to regular enrolment for those who are willing and selected.

But why? The armed forces wanted to keep trained soldiers longer, because four years is a short time to build combat experience.

ఇది చాలా important point.

Prelims trap: Agnipath has not been scrapped; it has been modified, and entry is still through the four-year Agniveer route.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

And the idea of a younger army is not new: the Kargil Review Committee of 1999 had flagged the need to lower the age profile of our forces. Mains debate: a younger army or a more experienced one?

మీ అభిప్రాయం comments లో రాయండి, and follow for one exam trap every day!
