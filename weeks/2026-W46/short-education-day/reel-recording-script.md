## Script

Who gave India its first IIT, the University Grants Commission and three national akademis?

ఒక్క మనిషే!

And today, November 11, is his birthday, National Education Day. Let's decode.

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad was independent India's first Education Minister, from 1947 to 1958, and since 2008 India marks his birthday as National Education Day.

Under him came the first Indian Institute of Technology at Kharagpur in 1951, the University Grants Commission in 1953, and the Sangeet Natak, Sahitya and Lalit Kala Akademis.

Before that, he was a fearless freedom fighter: he ran the Urdu journal Al-Hilal, became the youngest Congress president at 35 in 1923, and wrote India Wins Freedom. He received the Bharat Ratna, after his death, in 1992.

Prelims trap: three different days. National Education Day is November 11, for Azad; Teachers' Day is September 5, for Doctor Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan; and the United Nations' International Day of Education is January 24.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Azad dreamed of education for every child. Mains question: has the Right to Education turned enrolment into real learning?
