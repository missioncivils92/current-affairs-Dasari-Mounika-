## Script

The world needs about 300 billion dollars every year to protect people from climate change, but it is getting only about 30.

మరి మిగతా డబ్బు ఎక్కడ?

Let's decode.

UNEP's Adaptation Gap Report 2026, released on November 4 just before the climate summit in Antalya, is titled "Ready? Not." It says developing countries will need about 305 billion dollars a year for adaptation by 2035.

But what is adaptation? Mitigation means cutting emissions, so the planet warms less; adaptation means preparing for the warming that is already coming, like cyclone shelters, heat action plans and drought-proof crops.

Simple గా: mitigation అంటే తగ్గించడం, adaptation అంటే తట్టుకోవడం.

In 2024, developed countries gave only about 28 to 32 billion dollars in public adaptation finance, and even the goal from Belém to triple it by 2035 would cover only 20 to 29 per cent of the need.

Prelims trap: UNEP publishes two gap reports: the Emissions Gap Report is about cutting emissions, and the Adaptation Gap Report is about preparing for impacts. And UNEP is headquartered in Nairobi, in Kenya.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

For India, with its long coastline and rising heatwaves, adaptation is not a choice. Mains question: should adaptation finance come as grants from developed countries, or as loans?
