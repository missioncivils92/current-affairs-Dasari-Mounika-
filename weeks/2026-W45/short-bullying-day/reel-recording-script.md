## Script

**How it flows:** the scene → what the day is → how big the problem is → who protects children in India → traps → Mains and Ethics.

**📷 FULL FACE: *gentle, concerned.***

**What if** a child is scared of school, not because of exams, but because of messages on a phone?

🗣️ **ఈ రోజు ప్రపంచం అంతా దీని గురించి మాట్లాడుతోంది.**

Let's decode it the exam way.

**🎙️ *clear, "write this down".***

Today is the **International Day against Violence and Bullying at School, including Cyberbullying**, observed on the **first Thursday of November**. All **193 member states** of the **United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization** approved it in **2019**, and it was first observed in **2020**.

**🎙️ *serious, caring.***

**How big is the problem?** Its report found that **one in three** students was bullied at least once in the past month, and about **one in ten** has faced cyberbullying.

🗣️ **అంటే, మన class లో ప్రతి ముగ్గురిలో ఒక్కరు.**

**🎙️ *reassuring, firm.***

**So who protects our children in India?** The **National Commission for Protection of Child Rights**, a **statutory** body under a **2005** Act, watches over child rights; the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012**, punishes harassment even through digital means; and any cybercrime can be reported at **1930**.

**🎙️ *slower, trap voice.***

**Prelims trap:** this day was created by the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, headquartered in **Paris**, not by the General Assembly; and the Child Rights Commission is **statutory, not constitutional**.

🗣️ **ఇవి గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!**

**📷 FULL FACE: *warm, thoughtful.***

**Mains and Ethics question:** whose duty is it to stop cyberbullying, the school, the parents or the platform?

🗣️ **మీ అభిప్రాయం comments లో రాయండి**, and follow for one exam trap every day!
