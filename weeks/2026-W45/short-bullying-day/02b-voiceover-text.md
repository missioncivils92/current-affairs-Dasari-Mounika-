Today is the International Day against Violence and Bullying at School, including Cyberbullying, observed on the first Thursday of November. All 193 member states of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization approved it in 2019, and it was first observed in 2020.

How big is the problem? Its report found that one in three students was bullied at least once in the past month, and about one in 10 has faced cyberbullying. Ante, mana class lo prathi mugguru lo okkaru.

So who protects our children in India? The National Commission for Protection of Child Rights, a statutory body under a 2005 Act, watches over child rights; the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, punishes harassment even through digital means; and any cybercrime can be reported at 1930.
