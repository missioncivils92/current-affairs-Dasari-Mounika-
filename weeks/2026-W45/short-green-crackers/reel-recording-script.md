## How to record

- **Festive and curious:** it's Deepavali week, so smile and let the questions sound real.
- **Bold questions** ("What if…", "What makes them green?", "Who checks them?", "How can a court…") are the hooks. Ask each one, pause for half a second, then answer.
- **Telugu lines (🗣️):** say them slower and warmer.
- **Phone upright** at eye level, an arm's length away, with a little space above your head. Face the light and wipe the lens.
- **At the end, stay still and smile for 2 seconds** before you reach for the phone.
- **Send it here** at 720p (under 30 MB).

## Script

**The story in one line:** the order → what makes a cracker green → who checks it → why a court can limit a festival.

**📷 FULL FACE: *playful, curious.***

**What if** Diwali crackers were allowed for just 3 hours a day?

🗣️ **Delhi లో ఇదే జరిగింది.**

**So what exactly is a green cracker?**

**🎙️ *clear, news voice.***

On November 2nd, the Supreme Court allowed only **certified green crackers** in Delhi-NCR, on November 7th and 8th, from 6 to 7 in the morning and 8 to 10 at night, and none within **300 metres** of hospitals and schools.

**🎙️ *curious, then confident.***

**What makes them green?** CSIR-NEERI designed them: no **barium nitrate**, about **30%** less emissions, in three types, **SWAS, STAR and SAFAL**, with a green logo and **QR code**.

**🎙️ *"pay attention" voice.***

**And who checks them?** NEERI certifies the formula, and **PESO**, under the Commerce Ministry, licenses the makers under the Explosives Act.

**🎙️ *thoughtful.***

**But how can a court limit a festival?** It called crackers a religious practice, yet **Article 25** is subject to health, and **Article 21** gives us clean air.

🗣️ **ఇదే Mains point!**

**📷 FULL FACE: *warm, festive smile.***

🗣️ **Green గా దీపావళి జరుపుకుందాం!**

Follow for one exam trap every day.

*(Stay still and smile for 2 seconds.)*

**Pronunciation:** NEERI [NEE-ree] · PESO [PAY-so] · SWAS [Swaas] · SAFAL [Sa-PHAL]
