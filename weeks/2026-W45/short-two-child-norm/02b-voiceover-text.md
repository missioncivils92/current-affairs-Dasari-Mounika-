**🎙️ NARRATION**

This week, hearing a Maharashtra sarpanch's case, the Supreme Court said laws that disqualify people with more than two children have lost relevance, and sent its 2003 Javed judgment, which upheld such a law in Haryana, to a larger bench. Ante, final decision inka raaledu.

Why now? Because India's total fertility rate has fallen from about three in 2003 to 1.9, below the replacement level of 2.1. Rural India is at 2.1, cities at just 1.5.

But who makes such rules? Article 243F of the Constitution lets each state's law decide who is disqualified from panchayats, and the Court now asks whether punishing family size hurts reproductive choice under Article 21.

Ika mana Telugu states: Andhra Pradesh had this rule since 1994 and scrapped it in 2024, and Telangana removed it for rural local bodies in 2026, saying its rural fertility is just 1.7.
