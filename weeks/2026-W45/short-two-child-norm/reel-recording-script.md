## Script

What if having a third child could stop you from contesting a panchayat election?

ఇది చాలా states లో నిజంగా జరిగింది.

But this week the Supreme Court said such laws may have lost their relevance.

ఎందుకు? Let's decode.

This week, hearing a Maharashtra sarpanch's case, the Supreme Court said laws that disqualify people with more than two children have lost relevance, and sent its 2003 Javed judgment, which upheld such a law in Haryana, to a larger bench.

అంటే, final decision ఇంకా రాలేదు.

Why now? Because India's total fertility rate has fallen from about 3 in 2003 to 1.9, below the replacement level of 2.1. Rural India is at 2.1, cities at just 1.5.

But who makes such rules? Article 243F of the Constitution lets each state's law decide who is disqualified from panchayats, and the Court now asks whether punishing family size hurts reproductive choice under Article 21.

ఇక మన Telugu states: Andhra Pradesh had this rule since 1994 and scrapped it in 2024, and Telangana removed it for rural local bodies in 2026, saying its rural fertility is just 1.7.

Prelims trap: the Supreme Court has not struck down the two-child norm; it only referred the question to a larger bench.

ఇది గుర్తుపెట్టుకోండి!

Mains debate: should population policy use incentives or penalties?
