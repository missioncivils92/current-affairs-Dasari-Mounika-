🎥 It's eleven forty-seven. Your phone won't stop **buzzing**...

Today is the International Day against Violence and Bullying at School, including Cyberbullying.

It was declared by **UNESCO** in 2019, on a proposal by France, Mexico and Morocco — and first observed in **2020**.

It falls on the **first Thursday** of November — so the date changes every year.

Themes to remember — twenty twenty-one: cyberbullying. Twenty twenty-three: no place for fear. Twenty twenty-five: **screen smart**, safe in the digital era.

In India, CBSE schools observe it — and NCPCR has guidelines on bullying and cyberbullying.

**Prelims Trap.** UNESCO declared it — **not** the UN General Assembly.

🎥 [smile] Be the one who doesn't **forward** it.
