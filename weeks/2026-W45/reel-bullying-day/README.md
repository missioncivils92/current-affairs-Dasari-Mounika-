## Sources / verification
- UNESCO: day proclaimed by the 40th General Conference (2019); first Thursday of November — UNESCO website.
- “Almost one in three students has been bullied by peers at school at least once in the last month” — UNESCO, *Behind the Numbers: Ending School Violence and Bullying* (2019).
- BNS 2023 Sec 78 (stalking, incl. monitoring a woman's use of the internet/e-mail/electronic communication); IT Act 2000 Sec 66E, 67; UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in HEIs, 2009.
- Helplines: 1930 (National Cyber Crime Reporting, MHA), 14416 (Tele-MANAS, MoHFW), 1098 (Childline).
