## How to record this reel

- **Hold the phone upright (vertical)**, at eye level, about an arm's length away. Frame from the top of your head to the middle of your chest, with a little space above your head. Set the camera to 1080p, 30 fps.
- **Light in front of you**, a plain or tidy background, and a quiet room.
- **Record the whole script in one take**, looking into the lens, with energy and a smile, as if talking to one student.
- **Tone:** calm and neutral, like a teacher explaining a law. This is a pending court case, so no opinions.
- **The first and last lines are full-face.** For the middle lines, your face shows at the top of the screen while the map and zones play below. Keep your head near the top-centre of the frame.
- If you make a mistake, pause, say "retake", and read the sentence again.
- **Sending:** a 70-second video is usually 60–150 MB, too big for this chat. Upload it to Google Drive and send me the link (drive.google.com must be allowed in the environment's network settings). Alternatively, record at **720p** in your camera settings; a 70-second clip is then often under 30 MB and can be sent here directly.

## SHORT — UAPA bail


**📷 FULL FACE — *calm, curious.***

An undertrial has won a global human rights award, but the exam question is the law that decides his bail.

**🎙️ NARRATION**

In October, Dublin-based Front Line Defenders named Umar Khalid its Asia-Pacific laureate; he has been in custody since 2020 under UAPA in the Delhi riots conspiracy case, and denies the charges.

**🎙️ NARRATION — *slower.***

Section 43D, clause 5, of UAPA bars bail if the accusation looks prima facie true, and the Watali case of 2019 said courts need not weigh the evidence in detail.

**🎙️ NARRATION**

But K.A. Najeeb, 2021, held that long custody without trial can justify bail under Article 21; in January 2026, the Supreme Court still found a prima facie case and denied him bail.

Prelims trap: Front Line Defenders is an Irish NGO, not a UN body, and the 1998 UN Declaration on Human Rights Defenders is not a binding treaty.
