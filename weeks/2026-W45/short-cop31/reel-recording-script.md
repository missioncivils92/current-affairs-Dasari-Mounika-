## Script

**How it flows:** what it is → why India cares → what India will demand → what India itself promised → trap → Mains debate.

**📷 FULL FACE: *intriguing, curious.***

**What if** a meeting in Türkiye decides how hot your summers will be?

🗣️ **ఇదే నిజమే.**

From November 9th, the world meets in Antalya, *so what will India say there?* Let's decode.

**🎙️ *clear, explaining.***

**First, what is it?** It is COP 31, the **31st Conference of the Parties** to the **United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change**, signed in **1992**, *and here is a twist:* **Türkiye hosts it, but Australia runs the negotiations.**

**🎙️ *serious, caring.***

**But why should India care?** Because, a new United Nations-backed report says, **Himalayan glaciers could lose a third of their ice even at 1.5 degrees** of warming.

🗣️ **మన నదులు అన్నీ ఈ glaciers మీద ఆధారపడ్డాయి.**

**🎙️ *firm, confident.***

**So what will India demand?** On November 3rd, the Environment Minister said climate finance must be **public and affordable, not new debt**, and every country's targets must stay **nationally determined**, not imposed from the top.

**🎙️ *slower on the numbers.***

**And what has India itself promised?** By **2035**, a **47%** cut in emissions intensity from **2005** levels, and about **60%** of power capacity from non-fossil sources. **Prelims trap:** that is an **intensity target, not a cap on total emissions**, and our net zero year is **2070**.

**📷 FULL FACE: *thoughtful, inviting.***

**Mains debate:** should those who polluted first pay first?

🗣️ **మీ అభిప్రాయం comments లో రాయండి**, and follow for one exam trap every day!

*(Stay still and smile for 2 seconds.)*

**Pronunciation:** Antalya [An-TAAL-ya] · Türkiye [TUR-kee-yay] · Cryosphere [KRY-oh-sfeer]
