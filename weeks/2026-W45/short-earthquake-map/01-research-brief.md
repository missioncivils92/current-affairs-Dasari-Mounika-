# Research Brief — 2026-W45 Short: India's earthquake map (status check)

**Coverage:** status as of 1 Nov 2026 · **Release:** Tue 3 Nov 2026 (YouTube Short, about 70 s, on camera)
**Exam tags:** `#UPSC-Pre` `#UPSC-Mains-GS1` `#UPSC-Mains-GS3` `#APPSC-G1-Pre` `#TGPSC-G1-Pre` `#CAPF`

## Why this Short
The owner asked for a reel on the "new earthquake map of India". The research found the key point: **the new map is not in force.** The 2025 revision (with Zone VI) was notified in November 2025 and **withdrawn in March 2026**, and the 2016 code applies again. Coaching material from late 2025 still teaches Zone VI as current, so this is a live Prelims trap. That makes the Short's exam value the status itself, not the 2025 map.

## Visual notes
- India is drawn from the Survey of India outline (DataMeet).
- The Himalayan arc is a **schematic band**, labelled as such, because no zone boundary data was available.
- The Zone V pins mark the regions the code names; they are not boundaries.
