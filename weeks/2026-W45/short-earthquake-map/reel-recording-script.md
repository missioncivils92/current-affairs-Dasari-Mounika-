## SHORT — India's earthquake map


**📷 FULL FACE — *curious, bright.***

India got a new earthquake map with a brand-new Zone 6. So why is the answer in your exam still Zone 5?

**🎙️ NARRATION**

First, who makes this map? The Bureau of Indian Standards, under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, through its earthquake code, I-S 1893.

In November 2025 it released a new map. The old 2016 map has four zones; the new one added a sixth for the Himalayan arc, used probabilistic hazard modelling, moved boundary towns into the higher zone, and put about 61% of India in moderate to high hazard.

But in March 2026 the new code was withdrawn, after the Housing Ministry raised cost concerns, so the 2016 map is back in force.

And this is not a rumour: both the new map and its withdrawal were reported, so always check the date before you trust a map.

**🎙️ NARRATION — *slower.***

So India still has four zones: Zone 2, low; Zone 3, moderate; Zone 4, high; and Zone 5, very high.

**🎙️ NARRATION**

Zone 5 covers the North-East, parts of Kashmir, the Rann of Kutch, and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Prelims trap: there is no Zone 1, Zone 6 is not in force, and the zones are set by the B-I-S, not by the Earth Sciences Ministry, which only records earthquakes.

**📷 FULL FACE — *warm, smile.***
