> **Facts and status** (see `01-research-brief.md`):
> - **BIS** (Bureau of Indian Standards, under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution) sets the seismic zones in IS 1893.
> - The 2025 revision (Zone VI on the Himalayan arc, PSHA-based, boundary towns placed in the higher zone, about 61% of India in moderate-to-high hazard, as reported) was **withdrawn in March 2026**, after the Housing Ministry raised cost concerns (as reported).
> - **IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016** applies again: four zones, II–V. Zone I was merged into Zone II in 2002.
> - The **National Center for Seismology (Ministry of Earth Sciences)** records earthquakes; it does not set the zones.
> - **Never say Zone VI is in force.**
