## Short #1 — False election affidavits: any citizen can complain
- **Title (≤50 chars):** Can YOU prosecute a lying candidate? #UPSC #Shorts
- **On-screen hook text:** "Lied in the affidavit?"
- **Status:** verified (LiveLaw; Telangana Today). Ready to record.
- **Script (~115 words):**
> Can an ordinary voter take a lying candidate to court? This month, the Supreme Court said yes. On the sixteenth of October, a bench of Justices Dipankar Datta [Dee-pun-kar DUT-ta] and Sheel Nagu [Sheel NAA-goo] ruled on false election affidavits. Any private citizen can now file a criminal complaint against a candidate who hides or falsifies information. You do not have to wait for an official or the Election Commission. The provision — Section one-twenty-five A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. **Prelims Trap** — the penalty is up to six months in jail, a fine, or both. And magistrates can still reject frivolous complaints. Full weekly breakdown — every Sunday, six PM.
