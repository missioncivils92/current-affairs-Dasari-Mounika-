## Short #1 — False election affidavits: any citizen can complain
- **Title (≤50 chars):** Can a voter take a liar to court? #UPSC #Shorts
- **On-screen hook text:** "Lied in the affidavit?"
- **Status:** verified (LiveLaw; Telangana Today). **Publish Mon 2 Nov**, after Sunday's video reveals the MCQ challenge answer. Set Related video to the W43 masterclass.
- **Script (~115 words):**
> Can an ordinary voter take a lying candidate to court? This month, the Supreme Court said yes. On the sixteenth of October, a bench of Justices Dipankar Datta [Dee-pun-kar DUT-ta] and Sheel Nagu [Sheel NAA-goo] ruled on false election affidavits. A voter can now file a private criminal complaint against a candidate who hides or falsifies information. You do not have to wait for an official or the Election Commission. The provision — Section one-twenty-five A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. **Prelims Trap** — the penalty is up to six months in jail, a fine, or both. And magistrates can still reject frivolous complaints. Full breakdown in the video linked below.

## Short #2 — Russia–Ukraine: why no peace yet? (International Relations, GS-2)
- **Title (≤50 chars):** Russia–Ukraine: why no peace yet? #UPSC #Shorts
- **Publish:** Tue 27 Oct (the local truce starts that day). Record Mon 26 Oct night.
- **On-screen hook text:** "Why no peace yet?"
- **Save as:** `2026-W43_SHORT2_russia_ukraine.wav`
- **Script (~125 words, about 55 s):**
> Russia and Ukraine — why is there still no peace? The full-scale war began on the twenty-fourth of February, 2022. But the roots go back to 2014 — when Russia annexed Crimea [Kry-MEE-ya], and fighting began in the Donbas. Russia cites NATO's eastward expansion. Ukraine calls it an invasion of a sovereign state.

> The latest? There is still no full ceasefire. From the twenty-seventh of October, a local truce begins in Oleshky [Oh-LESH-kee], in the Kherson [Kher-SON] region — only to let aid in. New US-mediated talks are planned for November, in Abu Dhabi. The sticking points — territory in the Donbas, and security guarantees.

> **Prelims Trap** — Ukraine is not a NATO member. And India? It has abstained on UN votes, and calls for dialogue and diplomacy.
