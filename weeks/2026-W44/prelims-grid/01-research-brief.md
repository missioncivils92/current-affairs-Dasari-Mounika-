## 2. Pre-question for the cold open (from the Grid, UPSC format)

> Consider the following statements:
> 1. The Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement manages tuna stocks in the Indian Ocean.
> 2. India is a member of the Indian Ocean Tuna Commission.
> 3. The Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure is headquartered in Gurugram.
>
> How many of the above statements are correct? (a) Only one (b) Only two (c) All three (d) None

**Answer: (a) Only one.**
- Statement 2 is the correct one.
- Statement 1 is wrong: tuna is under the IOTC, not SIOFA.
- Statement 3 is wrong: the CDRI secretariat is in New Delhi; Gurugram hosts the ISA.

On screen it is held for 7 s over silence. The answer is revealed in the Grid at I1 and I3; say "the answer to the opening question" at I3.
