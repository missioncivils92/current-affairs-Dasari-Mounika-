## PRELIMS GRID — Prelims Grid: this week's static facts

Save as: `prelims-grid_VO_grid.wav`

**🎙️ NARRATION — *brisk, warm.***

Now the Prelims Grid: sixteen items from this week, and for each one, the static fact the examiner will actually mark, and the trap that comes with it.

**🎙️ NARRATION**

Cyclone Arnab crossed the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coast in the Godavari delta in the early hours of the twenty-eighth, as a very severe cyclonic storm.

A name is given only when a system reaches cyclonic-storm strength, which is why September's deep depression was never called Arnab; the name comes from Bangladesh's list.

**Prelims Trap.** Yanam sits inside Andhra Pradesh but belongs to the Union Territory of Puducherry, with Karaikal and Mahe.

On the fourteenth of October, the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved selling sixty point seven two per cent of IDBI Bank, the Government's and L-I-C's shares together, to a Fairfax company for about fifty-two thousand eight hundred crore rupees, subject to the R-B-I's approval.

**Prelims Trap.** IDBI is not a public sector bank: the R-B-I has classed it as a private sector bank since 2019, after L-I-C took majority control.

On the tenth of October, the R-B-I made banks keep a twenty per cent reserve, in cash with the R-B-I and earning no interest, on specified rupee forex derivative contracts above two million dollars.

It also cut, from one hundred million to five million dollars, the limit up to which users can deal without proving an underlying exposure.

**Prelims Trap.** These rules come under the Foreign Exchange Management Act of 1999, a civil law, not the older Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, under which violations were criminal offences.

On the twenty-first of October, Delhi's air slipped into the poor band, and Stage One of the Graded Response Action Plan was invoked across the National Capital Region.

The plan has four stages, from poor at two hundred and one to severe-plus above four hundred and fifty.

**Prelims Trap.** It is invoked by the Commission for Air Quality Management, a statutory body under a 2021 Act, not by the Central Pollution Control Board, which only publishes the index.

Vigilance Awareness Week runs from the twenty-sixth of October to the first of November, with the theme Probity for Prosperity, and it falls in the week of Sardar Patel's birth anniversary.

**Prelims Trap.** The Central Vigilance Commission began in 1964 by an executive resolution on the Santhanam Committee's advice, and became statutory only in 2003, so it is a statutory body, not a constitutional one.

India has approved joining the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement as a full party, after four years as a cooperating non-contracting party.

The agreement, signed in Rome in 2006, manages high-seas fish such as toothfish and alfonsino.

**Prelims Trap.** It excludes tuna, which belongs to a separate body, the Indian Ocean Tuna Commission, where India is already a member.

The Global Hunger Index 2026 ranks India one hundredth of one hundred and twenty-two, with a score of twenty-four point one, in the serious category; the Government has long disputed the index's method.

It combines four indicators, and three of them measure children: stunting, wasting and mortality.

**Prelims Trap.** It is published by aid organisations led by Welthungerhilfe, not by U-N agencies like the F-A-O or the W-F-P, whose hunger report is a different one.

Poland's Prime Minister visited India from the twenty-first to the twenty-third of October, and the two sides set a target to double trade by 2030, while Poland announced it will join the C-D-R-I.

**Prelims Trap.** The Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure was launched by India in 2019 with its secretariat in New Delhi, and it is not the International Solar Alliance, which began in 2015 and is headquartered in Gurugram.

The twelfth BRICS Parliamentary Forum met at Samvidhan Sadan in New Delhi on the twenty-sixth and twenty-seventh, chaired by the Lok Sabha Speaker, as India holds the BRICS chair this year.

**Prelims Trap.** BRICS now has ten full members, the original five plus Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, the U-A-E and Indonesia, and a question that adds Saudi Arabia to the list is the trap.

The biodiversity convention's seventeenth conference is meeting in Yerevan [Ye-re-VAAN], Armenia, until the thirtieth, for the first global review of the Kunming–Montreal [Kun-ming Mon-tree-awl] framework adopted in 2022.

**Prelims Trap.** That framework has twenty-three targets, including thirty by thirty, which means protecting thirty per cent of land and thirty per cent of sea by 2030, not thirty per cent combined.

The U-N Environment Programme's Emissions Gap Report 2026 projects about two point eight degrees of warming this century under current policies, and about two point six even if every national pledge is met.

**Prelims Trap.** The Emissions Gap Report is published by the U-N Environment Programme, not by the I-P-C-C and not by the climate convention's secretariat.

The U-N's Fund for responding to Loss and Damage made its first-ever allocation on the twenty-second of October: a provisional twenty million dollars for Nepal's flood recovery.

The fund was agreed at COP27 in Sharm el-Sheikh in 2022, made operational at COP28 in Dubai, and is hosted by the World Bank, with its Board based in the Philippines.

**Prelims Trap.** It is not the Green Climate Fund, which sits in Songdo, South Korea, and pays for mitigation and adaptation, not for loss and damage.

India's Ramsar sites now number one hundred and three, after Sukhna Wetland in Chandigarh and Sauj Jheel [Saunj Jheel] in Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh, were added on the fifteenth of October; India joined the 1971 convention in 1982.

**Prelims Trap.** The Montreux Record lists sites under threat, and India's two on it are Keoladeo and Loktak; Chilika was on it once but was removed in 2002.

The Ambaji temple's Mohanthal [MOHAN-thaal] prasad in Gujarat has reportedly received a geographical indication tag, joining the Tirupati laddu of Andhra Pradesh, which got its tag in 2009; the registry sits in Chennai, and Darjeeling tea was India's first G-I.

**Prelims Trap.** A G-I registration under the 1999 Act lasts ten years and can be renewed again and again, so it is not a permanent, one-time grant.

The Global Innovation Index 2026 keeps India thirty-eighth of one hundred and thirty-nine economies, with Switzerland, Sweden and the United States at the top, and India first among lower-middle-income economies.

**Prelims Trap.** This index is published by the World Intellectual Property Organization, not the World Economic Forum or U-N-C-T-A-D.

Three days to note: Infantry Day on the twenty-seventh marks the 1947 airlanding of Indian troops at Srinagar, World Savings Day is observed in India on the thirtieth, and Rashtriya Ekta Diwas on the thirty-first marks Sardar Patel's birth anniversary.

**Prelims Trap.** Most of the world marks Savings Day on the thirty-first, but India moved it to the thirtieth.
