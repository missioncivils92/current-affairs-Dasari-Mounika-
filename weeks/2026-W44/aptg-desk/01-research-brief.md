## 4. Visuals (v3, after three reviews of the v2 preview)

- **Notes-ready Glimpse kit** (shared with the Prelims Grid, `tools/masterclass-video/engine.js`), in teal. Every card ends on a frame the aspirant can pause and copy:
  - a labelled visual on the left;
  - a "write this down" table on the right, with the whole look-alike set (the High Court Articles, boards vs tribunals, the NSAP rates, the DM Act tiers, the lakes);
  - the PRELIMS TRAP bar below ("UPSC may write" → the fact).
- **The primary state document** is a row in each card's notes (or its credit line), not a floating strip.
- **Real imagery:**
  - D.6: Copernicus Sentinel-2 of the Godavari delta coast (8 Feb 2026), sharpened and contrast-stretched.
  - D.7: Kolleru (Jan–Apr 2026).
  - D.4 and D.5: terrain maps with the Krishna, Godavari, Tungabhadra, Bhima and Wainganga drawn from Natural Earth, plus state lines.
  - D.5: the Wardha and the Pranahita are drawn **schematically** (Natural Earth carries the lower course as "Wainganga"), and are credited so.
  - Every India frame carries the Survey of India outline. Interior state lines are drawn only inside it, and never for J&K, Ladakh or Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Text:** every on-screen label is at least 24 px at 1080p (§6.2), and nothing zooms, so a paused frame stays sharp.
- **The Telugu States sheet** (D.R) is the Desk page of the PDF.
- **The quiz card** shows the statements, the stem and the options at full opacity. (v2 had a bug that left the stem at 60% and the options at 40%.)
