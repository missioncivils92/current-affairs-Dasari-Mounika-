**Trap Vault item (Statement format):**
> Consider the following statements:
> 1. Kherson oblast is part of the Donbas region.
> 2. The Kerch Strait joins the Sea of Azov to the Black Sea.

## 2. Black Sea map card (PDF, one page; reused all year for this story arc)

One static map, every label on the map, India-compliant boundaries per `CLAUDE.md` §6.1. Each line is a testable fact. Verify the starred lines against a primary source before printing.

| Set | Facts |
|---|---|
| **Black Sea littoral states (6)** | Türkiye, Bulgaria, Romania, Ukraine, Russia, Georgia. Look-alikes that are **not** littoral: Moldova, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Greece |
| **Strait chain** | Black Sea → **Bosphorus** → Sea of Marmara (a sea) → **Dardanelles** → Aegean Sea. **Kerch Strait**: Sea of Azov ↔ Black Sea |
| **Treaty regime** | Montreux Convention (1936) governs the Turkish Straits only. Kerch was covered by a 2003 Russia–Ukraine treaty, denounced by both in 2023; its status is now disputed |
| **Rivers** | Into the Black Sea: Danube, Dniester, Southern Bug, Dnipro. Into the Sea of Azov: Don, Kuban. Contrast trap: Volga → Caspian |
| **Sea of Azov** | World's shallowest sea, average depth about 7 m |
| **Ukraine's land neighbours (7)** | Russia, Belarus, Poland, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Moldova (Bulgaria, Latvia and Lithuania do not border Ukraine) |
| **Regions in news** | Donbas = Donetsk + Luhansk. Kherson and Zaporizhzhia are **not** Donbas. Crimea is a peninsula. ZNPP is at Enerhodar on the Dnipro (former Kakhovka reservoir), not on the Don |
| **India bridge** | ZNPP has 6 × VVER-1000 reactors, the same Russian reactor family as Kudankulam* |
| **River-bank rule** | Right and left banks are named facing downstream (Kherson: right bank; Oleshky: left bank). Apply it to the Ganga's tributaries* |
