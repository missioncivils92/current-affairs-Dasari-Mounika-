## How to use these notes

- **Watch the videos first, then read these notes.** This week has two: the **Prelims Grid** (16 topics) and the **AP–TG Desk** (7 Telugu States topics).
- **Each topic has three parts:** why it is in the news, the static facts the examiner marks, and the Prelims trap.
- **Practise:** attempt the 25 MCQs in Part C *before* you check the answer key in Part D.
- **Revise:** the one-page sheet in Part E is your week on a page.
- **Currency:** facts are as of **1 Nov 2026**. Items marked *reported* were not yet confirmed on the official page, so check them before quoting.

> **The examiner's three tricks this week.** Every trap below is one of three moves.
>
> 1. **Swap the body or the ministry.** DIPAM placed in Commerce · GRAP "invoked by the CPCB" · the GII "by the WEF" · the Emissions Gap "by the IPCC" · GI "under Culture" · NSAP "under Social Justice".
> 2. **Move the place or the date.** Yanam placed "in Andhra Pradesh" · the CDRI placed "in Gurugram" · Savings Day moved to "31 October" · the barrage put "on the Godavari main stem".
> 3. **Stretch the set or the number.** 30×30 "combined" · Saudi Arabia added "to BRICS" · Chilika kept "on the Montreux Record" · Article 243D(6) read as "mandatory".

## Part A · Prelims Grid: 16 topics `UPSC-Pre · Group-1`

### 1. Cyclone Arnab `GS-1 · GS-3 · APPSC`

- **Why in news:** Very Severe Cyclonic Storm (VSCS) **Arnab** crossed the Andhra Pradesh–Yanam coast in the Godavari delta on **28 Oct 2026**, between Narsapur and Yanam (IMD position, as reported).
- **How a cyclone forms:** warm sea water (≥ 26.5–27 °C), enough **Coriolis force** (none at the Equator, so no cyclones within about 5°), weak vertical wind shear, and a pre-existing low.
- **How cyclones are named:**
  - **13 countries** on the WMO/ESCAP Panel give 13 names each, so the list has **169 names** (adopted 2020).
  - **RSMC New Delhi (IMD)** gives the next name once a storm reaches **62 km/h** (Cyclonic Storm).
  - A name is never repeated. "Arnab" came from **Bangladesh's** list.
- **IMD scale (km/h):**

| Class | CS | SCS | VSCS | ESCS | SuCS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wind speed | 62–88 | 89–117 | 118–166 | 167–221 | ≥ 222 |

- **Trap:** ❌ "Yanam is part of Andhra Pradesh." ✅ Yanam belongs to the **UT of Puducherry**, which has four parts: Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam.

### 2. IDBI Bank sale `GS-3 · RBI Grade B`

- **Why in news:** on **14 Oct**, the **CCEA** cleared the sale of **60.72%** of IDBI Bank (GoI 30.48% + LIC 30.24%) to a Fairfax company, for about **₹52,878 crore**. RBI approval is pending.
- **How a strategic sale works:** **DIPAM** (Ministry of **Finance**) runs it → the **CCEA** (chaired by the PM) approves it → the **RBI** checks that the buyer is "fit and proper" to control a bank.
- **History:** 1964 IDBI Act (as an RBI subsidiary) → 1976 transferred to GoI → 2004 IDBI Bank.
- **Trap:** ❌ "IDBI is a public sector bank." ✅ The RBI has classed it as a **private sector bank since January 2019**, after LIC took majority control.

### 3. RBI's Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve `GS-3 · RBI Grade B`

- **Why in news:** on **10 Oct**, the RBI asked banks to keep a **20% reserve**, in **cash with the RBI** and earning **no interest**, on specified rupee forex derivative contracts **above $2 mn**. Separately, the limit for dealing **without proving an underlying exposure** was cut from **$100 mn to $5 mn**.
- **FERA vs FEMA:**

| | FERA 1973 | FEMA 1999 |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Criminal** | **Civil** (penalties, compounding) |
| In force | 1974 | **1 June 2000** |
| Enforced by | Enforcement Directorate | Enforcement Directorate |

- **Trap:** ❌ "These rules come under FERA." ✅ **FEMA**. FERA was repealed, and forex violations are no longer criminal offences.

### 4. Delhi's air: GRAP Stage I `GS-3`

- **Why in news:** Delhi's AQI reached **244 (poor)** on **21 Oct**, and **GRAP Stage I** was invoked across the NCR.
- **AQI bands:** Good 0–50 · Satisfactory 51–100 · Moderate 101–200 · **Poor 201–300** · Very Poor 301–400 · Severe 401+.
- **GRAP stages:** I = poor · II = very poor · III = severe · IV = severe+ (above **450**).
- **Two bodies:** GRAP is invoked by the **CAQM**, a statutory body under a **2021 Act**. The **CPCB** publishes the AQI, which was launched in 2014 and tracks **8 pollutants**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The CPCB invokes GRAP." ✅ The **CAQM** invokes it; the CPCB only publishes the index.

### 5. Vigilance Awareness Week `GS-2 · GS-4`

- **Why in news:** the CVC is marking the week from **26 Oct to 1 Nov** (it contains Sardar Patel's birth anniversary on 31 Oct). The theme is **"Probity for Prosperity"**.
- **About the CVC:**
  - Set up in **1964** by executive resolution, on the **Santhanam Committee's** advice.
  - **Statutory** since the **CVC Act, 2003**.
  - Made up of the CVC plus up to 2 Vigilance Commissioners, each serving 4 years or until age 65.
  - Selected by the **PM, the Home Minister and the Leader of the Opposition**, and appointed by the **President**.
- **Look-alike set:** **Lokpal** (2013 Act) · **CBI** (executive, DSPE Act 1946) · **CAG** (the only **constitutional** one, Art 148).
- **Trap:** ❌ "The CVC is a constitutional body." ✅ It is **statutory** (2003 Act).

### 6. India joins SIOFA `GS-2 · GS-3`

- **Why in news:** in mid-October, India approved becoming a **full party** to the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement. It had been a cooperating non-party since 2022.
- **Three ocean bodies:**

| Body | Covers | Seat |
|---|---|---|
| **SIOFA** (signed 2006, in force 2012) | **Non-tuna** fish on the high seas | Réunion |
| **IOTC** (an FAO body; India is a member) | **Tuna** | Seychelles |
| **CCAMLR** | Antarctic marine life | Hobart |

- **Trap:** ❌ "SIOFA manages tuna." ✅ Tuna is the job of the **IOTC**.

### 7. Global Hunger Index 2026 `GS-2 · Social`

- **Why in news:** India is ranked **100 of 122**, with a score of **24.1**, in the **"serious"** category. The Government disputes the method.
- **How it is weighted:** undernourishment **1/3** · child mortality **1/3** · child stunting **1/6** · child wasting **1/6**. A score of 20–35 counts as **serious**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The GHI is published by the FAO or the WFP." ✅ It comes from **aid organisations led by Welthungerhilfe**. The FAO and its partners publish a different report, the **SOFI**.

### 8. Poland joins the CDRI `GS-2`

- **Why in news:** Poland's Prime Minister visited India on **21–23 Oct**. The two sides aim to **double trade by 2030**, and Poland **announced** it will join the CDRI. India and Poland have been strategic partners since August 2024.
- **India-led alliances:**

| Alliance | Launched | Seat |
|---|---|---|
| **ISA** | 2015, COP21 Paris (with France) | **Gurugram** |
| **CDRI** | 2019, UN Climate Action Summit, **New York** | **New Delhi** |
| **Global Biofuels Alliance** | 2023, G20 New Delhi | — |
| **International Big Cat Alliance** | 2023 (50 years of Project Tiger) | — |

- **Trap:** ❌ "The CDRI sits in Gurugram." ✅ The **CDRI is in New Delhi**; the **ISA is in Gurugram**.

### 9. 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum `GS-2`

- **Why in news:** held at **Samvidhan Sadan, New Delhi**, on **26–27 Oct**, and chaired by the **Lok Sabha Speaker**. India holds the BRICS chair in 2026.
- **The 10 full members:** BRIC (2009) · South Africa (2010) · Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE (2024) · Indonesia (2025). The New Development Bank (NDB) is headquartered in **Shanghai**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "Saudi Arabia is a full member." ✅ It was invited but **has not confirmed**.

### 10. CBD COP17, Yerevan `GS-3`

- **Why in news:** COP17 is in **Yerevan, Armenia**, from **19 to 30 Oct**. It is the first global review of the **Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF)**.
- **The convention family:**
  - **CBD:** Rio, 1992 · secretariat in Montreal · the **USA is not a party** · India hosted **COP11 in Hyderabad (2012)**.
  - **Cartagena Protocol** (2000): biosafety.
  - **Nagoya Protocol** (2010): access and benefit-sharing.
  - **KMGBF** (2022): **23 targets**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "30×30 means 30% of land and sea combined." ✅ It means **30% of land AND 30% of sea, each, by 2030**.

### 11. UNEP Emissions Gap Report `GS-3`

- **Why in news:** the report, released **21 Oct**, projects about **2.8 °C** of warming under current policies, and about **2.6 °C** even if every pledge is met.
- **Who publishes what:** **UNEP:** Emissions Gap and Adaptation Gap · **IPCC:** Assessment Reports · **WMO:** State of the Global Climate · **UNFCCC:** NDC Synthesis Report.
- **India's targets:** emissions intensity **45% lower** than 2005 by 2030 · **50%** of power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030 · **net zero by 2070**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The Emissions Gap Report is the IPCC's." ✅ It is **UNEP's**.

### 12. Loss and Damage Fund: first allocation `GS-3 · GS-2`

- **Why in news:** on **22 Oct**, the Fund's Board made its **first-ever allocation**: **$20 mn (provisional)** to **Nepal** for recovery from the August floods.
- **About the fund (FRLD):** agreed at **COP27 (2022)** and made operational at **COP28 (2023)**. It is hosted by the World Bank, and its Board sits in the Philippines.
- **The other climate funds:** **GCF** (Cancún 2010; based in **Songdo, South Korea**) · **GEF** (1991) · **Adaptation Fund** (Kyoto Protocol).
- **Trap:** ❌ "Loss and damage is paid from the Green Climate Fund." ✅ It has **its own fund, the FRLD**. The GCF pays for mitigation and adaptation.

### 13. Ramsar sites reach 103 `GS-3 · Places`

- **Why in news:** on **15 Oct**, India reached **103** Ramsar sites. The new ones are **Sukhna Wetland (Chandigarh)** and **Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri, UP)**.
- **Four Ramsar facts:**
  - The convention was signed at **Ramsar, Iran, on 2 Feb 1971**; India joined in **1982**.
  - India's first sites were **Chilika and Keoladeo** (1981).
  - India's **Montreux Record** (sites under threat): **Keoladeo** (1990) and **Loktak** (1993).
  - India's largest site is the **Sundarbans**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "Chilika is on the Montreux Record." ✅ It was **removed in 2002**.

### 14. A new GI tag (reported) `GS-1 · GS-3`

- **Why in news:** **Ambaji's Mohanthal** (a temple prasad from Gujarat) has **reportedly** received a GI tag. Confirm it in the GI Journal before quoting.
- **The GI law:**
  - GI of Goods (Registration and Protection) **Act 1999**, in force **2003**; it implements TRIPS Arts 22–24.
  - Registered by the **GI Registry, Chennai**, under the CGPDTM, DPIIT, **Ministry of Commerce**.
  - Valid for **10 years** and renewable any number of times.
  - India's first GI was **Darjeeling tea**.
- **Telugu States GIs:** Tirupati Laddu · Pochampally Ikat · Araku Valley coffee · Banganapalle mango.
- **Trap:** ❌ "A GI tag is a permanent, one-time grant" or "it is under the Culture Ministry." ✅ It lasts **10 years, renewable**, and sits under **Commerce**.

### 15. Global Innovation Index 2026 `GS-3 · RBI Grade B`

- **Why in news:** India is **38th of 139** economies, and **1st among lower-middle-income** economies. Switzerland, Sweden and the USA top the list.
- **Who publishes what:** **GII:** WIPO (Geneva) · **World Investment Report:** UNCTAD · **Global Gender Gap Report:** WEF · **Human Development Report:** UNDP.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The GII is published by the WEF." ✅ It is published by **WIPO**.

### 16. Three days to note `GS-1`

- **27 Oct, Infantry Day:** marks 1947, when troops of **1 Sikh** were air-landed at **Srinagar**.
- **30 Oct, World Savings Day (India):** the day began in Milan in 1924. Most countries mark it on 31 Oct; **India marks it on 30 Oct**.
- **31 Oct, Rashtriya Ekta Diwas:** marked since 2014 on **Sardar Patel's** birth anniversary. His **Statue of Unity** (182 m) stands at **Ekta Nagar, on the Narmada**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "India marks World Savings Day on 31 Oct." ✅ India marks it on **30 Oct**.

## Part B · AP–TG Desk: 7 Telugu States topics `APPSC · TGPSC Group-1`

### 1. AP's BC quota in local bodies `GS-2 · APPSC`

- **Why in news:** on **1 Oct**, the **AP High Court** set aside the orders giving **34%** BC reservation in rural local bodies. On **9 Oct**, the **Supreme Court** dismissed the State's appeal. The AP Cabinet has since adopted its dedicated commission's report.
- **The triple test** (*Vikas Kishanrao Gawali*, 2021):
  1. a **dedicated commission** studies backwardness;
  2. the quota is fixed **body by body** on that data;
  3. total SC + ST + OBC reservation stays **≤ 50%**.
- **The judgment chain:** *Indra Sawhney* 1992 (50% ceiling) → *K. Krishna Murthy* 2010 → *Gawali* 2021.
- **Trap:** ❌ "Article 243D makes a BC quota in panchayats mandatory." ✅ **Art 243D(6)** (panchayats) and **243T(6)** (municipalities) are **enabling, not mandatory**.

### 2. Telangana HC: acting Chief Justice `GS-2 · TGPSC`

- **Why in news:** **Justice P. Sam Koshy** is the acting Chief Justice of the Telangana High Court, after the Chief Justice was elevated to the Supreme Court.
- **The High Court Articles:** **214** a High Court for each State · **217** appointment of judges · **223** acting Chief Justice · **224** additional and acting judges · **224A** retired judges · **231** one High Court for two or more States.
- Telangana has had its **own High Court since 1 Jan 2019**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The CJI appoints the acting Chief Justice." ✅ The **President** does, under **Art 223**.

### 3. Who pays AP's pension? `GS-2 · Telugu States Lens`

- **Why in news:** under **NTR Bharosa**, the State pays an elderly pensioner **₹4,000 a month**. Of this, the Centre's share is only **₹200** (under IGNOAPS), about one-twentieth.
- **NSAP**, run by the **Ministry of Rural Development**, pays:
  - **IGNOAPS:** ₹200 a month at ages 60–79, ₹500 from age 80;
  - **IGNWPS** (widows): ₹300;
  - **IGNDPS** (disability): ₹300;
  - **NFBS** (family benefit): ₹20,000 once;
  - **Annapurna:** 10 kg of free grain a month.
- **Trap:** ❌ "NSAP is run by the Social Justice Ministry." ✅ It is run by **Rural Development**.
- **Group-1 Mains practice:** *Describe the State's social security pensions and the role of central assistance.*

### 4. Krishna and Godavari river boards `GS-1 · GS-2`

- **Why in news:** the **KRMB** (26 Oct) and **GRMB** (30 Oct) were scheduled to meet, with the **Polavaram backwater** on the GRMB agenda.
- **Boards vs tribunals:**

| Body | Basis |
|---|---|
| KRMB, GRMB | AP Reorganisation Act 2014, **s.85** |
| Apex Council (Union Jal Shakti Minister + both CMs) | APRA 2014, s.84 |
| Polavaram (a **national project**) | APRA 2014, s.90 |
| Water tribunals | **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956**, under **Art 262** |
| KWDT-I / KWDT-II / GWDT | Report 1973 / 2010 / 1980 |

- **Remember it as:** boards manage, tribunals decide.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The river boards are Art 262 tribunals." ✅ They are bodies created by **APRA 2014**.

### 5. Tummidihatti barrage `GS-1 · TGPSC`

- **Why in news:** on **26 Oct**, Telangana's CM held talks in Mumbai. Telangana wants a full reservoir level of **152 m**, against the **148 m** it says was agreed in 2016. The two States agreed to form a **joint committee of officials**.
- **The river:** the **Pranahita** = **Wardha + Wainganga**. It runs along the Telangana–Maharashtra border and joins the **Godavari at Kaleshwaram**.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The barrage is on the Godavari main stem." ✅ It is on the **Pranahita**, a tributary.

### 6. Who runs a disaster response? `GS-3 · APPSC`

- **Why in news:** Andhra Pradesh ran its response to Cyclone Arnab through its **State Disaster Management Authority**.
- **The Disaster Management Act, 2005:**
  - **NDMA** (s.3): chaired by the **Prime Minister**;
  - **SDMA** (s.14): chaired by the **Chief Minister**;
  - **DDMA** (s.25): chaired by the **Collector**;
  - **NDRF:** set up under **s.44**.
- **Warnings:** the IMD is the **RSMC New Delhi** for the north Indian Ocean.
- **Trap:** ❌ "The SDMA is chaired by the Governor." ✅ The **Chief Minister** chairs it.

### 7. Kolleru: a sanctuary's limits `GS-3 · APPSC`

- **Why in news:** Andhra Pradesh has asked the Supreme Court's **Central Empowered Committee** to exclude about **20,000 acres** from the Kolleru Wildlife Sanctuary (*as reported*).
- **The lakes, as a set:**

| Lake | Type | State | Ramsar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kolleru** | **Freshwater**, between the Krishna and Godavari deltas | AP | 2002 |
| Chilika | Brackish lagoon | Odisha | 1981 |
| Pulicat | Brackish lagoon | AP–Tamil Nadu | (not covered here) |
| Sambhar | Saline | Rajasthan | 1990 |
| Loktak | Freshwater, with phumdis | Manipur | 1990 |

- **Trap:** ❌ "Kolleru is a brackish lagoon like Chilika." ✅ Kolleru is **freshwater**.
