## 3. Five-step analysis

**Step 1: Trigger.** On 14 Oct 2026 the Union Cabinet approved:
- the continuation of NSAP for FY 2026-27 to 2030-31;
- an outlay of ₹53,428 crore;
- the new name Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana;
- the renaming of four components;
- a relaxed disability pension.

Pivot: eligibility widened, but adequacy did not; the Centre's old-age rate has been ₹200 since 2007.

**Step 2: Foundations.**
- **Terms:** non-contributory social assistance; benchmark disability (RPwD Act 2016, s.2(r), at least 40%); central assistance and State top-up.
- **Roots:**
  - Art 41 (DPSP) and Art 37 (non-justiciable).
  - RPwD Act 2016 s.24(1) (+25%).
  - India ratified the UNCRPD in 2007.
  - Nodal ministry: MoRD.
  - BPL eligibility; Gram Panchayats and municipalities identify beneficiaries.

**Step 3: The chain.**
- The 80% bar and the age-18 floor shut out children and moderately disabled adults. **Therefore** the bar is aligned to the Act's 40% and to birth, and coverage grows about 3×.
- **But** the old-age, widow and family-benefit rates are frozen, the outlay of about ₹10,700 crore a year is close to past spending, and the States (AP ₹4,000) carry most of the cost.
- **Therefore** the next test is the guidelines (due within three months) and identification (CAG 2023).

**Step 4a: Traps** (covering the whole look-alike set: all five components and all current rates).
1. UPSC may ask whether the renaming raised all pensions. The trap: only the disability rates changed (₹375/₹625). Old age stays ₹200 (60–79) and ₹500 (80+), widow ₹300, NFBS ₹20,000 one-time.
2. UPSC may ask for the nodal ministry. The trap: MoRD, not MoSJE or DEPwD.
3. UPSC may ask how many components were renamed. The trap: four of five, with Annapurna unchanged. The maternity benefit (NMBS) left NSAP in 2001.
4. UPSC may ask about the constitutional basis. The trap: Art 41 (DPSP), not Art 21 or a fundamental right. Also not Art 39 or Art 47.
5. UPSC may pair "40%" with the RPwD Act. The trap: 40% is the *benchmark disability* in s.2(r); the +25% rule is s.24(1). Don't swap the two sections.

**Step 4b: Mains Lens.** Hub row 1.12 is the compact version; the full version is `[EXT]` row 1.12a.

**Step 5: Way forward.**
- **Raise the amounts.** The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Rural Development (2025 report; action-taken report tabled 2 Apr 2026) recommended a revised Cabinet proposal for a substantial increase in pensions [VERIFY the report number].
- **Fix identification.** CAG Report No. 10 of 2023 recommends periodic surveys of eligible people, social audits and working grievance redress.
- **Index the amounts.** Link the rates to a rule, as s.24 already does for disability. This is framed as an option, not as a cited recommendation.
- **India's imperative:** an ageing society needs a floor that keeps its value.

**Model question:** "The Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana widens eligibility but not adequacy. Critically examine." (15 marks, 250 words, GS-2)

- **Conclusion:** indexation, updated beneficiary lists, and the guidelines within three months.
