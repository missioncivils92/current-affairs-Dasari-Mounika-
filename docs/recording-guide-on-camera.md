## What changes

You record the **whole script on camera**, just as you read it now. **I choose when your face appears**, following the layout written in the script:

| In the script | What viewers see | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **[FACE]** | You, full screen, with your name below | Opening, warm-up question, moving between topics, the three tricks, closing |
| **[SPLIT]** | You on the left, the slide on the right | Topic title slides |
| **[CORNER]** | A small circle of you in the corner | Long explanation slides |
| *(no tag)* | The slide full screen, with your voice | News, tables, maps, traps, MCQs, notes |

Your face will be on screen for about 15–20% of the video. Students read and copy everything else full screen.

## Setting up (5 minutes)

1. **Phone:** horizontal, at eye level, about an arm's length away, on a stand or a pile of books. Set the camera to **1080p, 30 fps**.
2. **Light:** a window or lamp **in front of you**, never behind you.
3. **Background:** plain or tidy. A bookshelf looks good.
4. **Sound:** a wired or lapel mic if you have one. Choose a quiet room with the fan off.
5. **Eyes:** look into the **camera lens**, not the screen. Keep the script just below the lens.

## While recording

- Read the script in order, in a few parts. For example, Grid topics 1–8, then 9–16, then the AP–TG Desk.
- If you make a mistake, **pause, say "retake"**, and read the whole sentence again. I cut it out.
- **No need to pause at the questions.** I add the countdowns.
- Smile on the opening, the tricks and the closing. Those are the moments on camera.

## Sending the videos

Video files are big, so **upload them to Google Drive** and send me the share link ("Anyone with the link"). For me to download them, **drive.google.com** must be allowed in the environment's network settings. Name the files `grid_part1.mp4`, `grid_part2.mp4`, `desk.mp4`, and so on.
