# DRAFT: Story selection and weekly format rules (for owner review)

**Status:** APPROVED WITH CHANGES on 28 Oct 2026 (decisions 28–29) and now in force in `CLAUDE.md` and `PLAYBOOK.md`, which are the authoritative text. Changes from this draft: up to 3 Deep Dives in a heavy week (the 3rd needs EYS ≥ 8); the Update Desk is optional; the consensus check is a flag only; the AP–TG Desk stays 3 min weekly until 24 Jan 2027. This file is kept as the record of the proposal.
**Date:** 28 Oct 2026
**Why:** five independent exam-value reviews of the Oleshky sample (`docs/research/reviews/2026-W44-exam-value/`) agreed that the video was low-yield for exam study. They traced the root cause to **story selection, not production**:
- Oleshky was picked on its event day, before the week was swept.
- The "pass 2 of 5" test is a minimum bar, not a ranking.
- The weekly format spends most of its minutes on depth, while the first audience (Prelims) needs breadth.

**Already decided by the owner (28 Oct):**
- Breadth-first hub (§C below).
- Oleshky archived as a 45-second Update.
- The next sample is chosen from a real triage table.

**Waiting for approval:** the selection rules (§A, §B) and the wording of everything here.

| Area | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Choosing stories | Pass 2 of 5 yes/no criteria | A **0–10 exam-yield score (EYS)** for every candidate, plus a mandatory ranked table |
| Deep Dive entry | "High exam yield" (undefined) | **EYS ≥ 7**, with an India link and durability; at most 2 Deep Dives |

| MCQs on screen | Mixed formats; answers sometimes unstated | UPSC formats only; **answer always stated, with a reason** |
| Numbers on revision cards | Roll-up animations | **Final values only** |

## A. Exam-yield score (EYS): replaces CLAUDE.md §3 item 3 ("Selection test")

> 3. **Exam-yield score (EYS).** Score every candidate from 0 to 10 in the research brief's triage table **before** assigning any tier.
>    - **P · Prelims yield (0–3).** Count the distinct testable static facts that *arise from this week's event*: a new Act or section, a scheme and its nodal ministry, a report and its publisher, a designated site, species or place, a body's membership or composition, a treaty step, a "first".
>      - 0 = none; 1 = one; 2 = two or three; 3 = four or more, or a complete look-alike set.
>      - **Borrowed anchors score 0.** These are facts you could teach in any week, whatever the news.
>    - **M · Mains yield (0–2).** 2 = a recurring Mains theme with a verified past-question precedent; 1 = plausible; 0 = commentary only.
>    - **I · India-centricity (0–2).** 2 = an Indian law, court, institution, scheme or policy is the actor or the object; 1 = a direct, measurable effect on India (its citizens, prices, a treaty India is party to, a vote India cast); 0 = India is a bystander.
>    - **D · Durability (0–2).** 2 = still examinable in May 2027 without updating; 1 = a process with known next steps; 0 = episodic.
>    - **S · State exams (0–1).** A genuine AP/TG link that APPSC or TGPSC Group-1 could examine.
>
>    Write it as `EYS 8 (P3·M2·I2·D1·S0)`, with a one-line reason per component.
>    - Below 3: drop the item, or make it a Short.
>    - **No story is tiered from memory, from tracker momentum, or from last week's plan.**
>
> 3a. **Consensus check.** For each Deep Dive candidate, record whether it appears in The Hindu or Indian Express, in PIB, and in at least two UPSC daily digests that week. A Deep Dive that appears in none of these needs a written reason in the brief, approved by Mounika.

**Applied to Oleshky:** P1 (GC IV Art 17) · M1 · I0 · D0 · S0 = **EYS 2**, which makes it an Update at most.

## B. Tiers and escalation: replaces the "When to use" column of CLAUDE.md §3.1 and its escalation signals

> | Tier | Entry rule |
> |---|---|
> | **Deep Dive** | EYS ≥ 7 **and** I ≥ 1 **and** D ≥ 1 **and** P ≥ 2. At most **2** in the hub. At least **one** must have I = 2 (an Indian institution or policy at its centre). At most **one** may be international, and only if India's policy, its citizens or a treaty obligation is directly engaged this week. |
> | **Focus** | EYS 5–6, or a Deep Dive candidate that loses on runtime. 0–1 in the hub. |
> | **Glimpse** | EYS 3–4 with P ≥ 1, or any EYS ≥ 5 item whose value is mainly a Prelims set. **Target 18–22 a week.** Never fewer than 15, unless the triage table shows fewer items that qualify. |
> | **Update** | Score the **change only** (the delta). It becomes a Deep Dive only if the delta alone scores EYS ≥ 7. The tracker's status never decides the tier. |
>
> **Escalation signals** (each still needs the EYS thresholds):
> - an SC or HC judgment, or a Constitution Bench order;
> - a new or amended law, rule or scheme;
> - India signing, joining, ratifying or leaving a treaty or body;
> - a disaster in India or its neighbourhood with a geographic lesson;
> - an RBI, Budget or trade decision;
> - a foreign event **that changes Indian policy or directly affects Indian citizens**.
>
> A local development in a foreign conflict (a truce, an offensive, a town captured) is a Glimpse or an Update, unless India is a party.
>
> **Arc fatigue.** The same story arc may not supply a Deep Dive in two consecutive weeks unless its delta scores EYS ≥ 8.

## C. Breadth-first weekly hub: replaces the CLAUDE.md §4.1 segment table, typical week and cascade (owner-approved shape; wording for review)

> | # | Segment | Target | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Cold open | 0:45 | Inside the week's top story. The **pre-question** comes from the Prelims Grid, in a UPSC format (never yes/no). |
> | 2 | Update Desk | 1:30 | 3–5 tracker deltas × 20–30 s. Only changes that alter what the viewer wrote down ("Last time we said X; now it's Y"). Skip it if nothing moved. |
> | 3 | **Prelims Grid: National · International · Misc** | **8:00** | 14–16 Glimpses × 25–35 s: what happened → the static set → one trap. **Starts by 2:30.** One card per item, reused as-is in the PDF. |
> | 4 | Deep Dive 1 (I = 2) | 6:00–6:30 | The 5 steps (§4.2), compressed. `[EXT]` rows go to Decode Extended or the PDF. |
> | 5 | **AP–TG Desk** (Local: Telugu States) | **3:00** | Its own chapter, **fixed every week until 24 Jan 2027**. 6–7 Glimpses: polity and courts → schemes and budget → rivers, projects and disasters → indices, persons and firsts. A primary state document (GO no., gazette, budget page) on each card. Close with one Group-1-style question on screen. |
> | 6 | Deep Dive 2 | 5:00–5:30 | As above. |
> | 7 | Focus / Telugu States Lens | 0–1:30 | Only when an EYS 5–6 item has a Mains angle, or a Deep Dive has a genuine AP/TG link. |
> | 8 | Trap Vault | 2:00 | 10 items in "how many", Statement-I/II and pairs formats: this week's traps, 2 from last week and 1 from four weeks ago. |
> | 9 | Verdict + CTA | 0:45 | |
>
> **Typical week (29:00):** 0:45 · 1:30 · 8:00 · 6:15 · 3:00 · 5:15 · 1:30 · 2:00 · 0:45.
> - The runtime limits in §2 are unchanged: 26–30 min (default about 28), and 35 min is the ceiling.
> - **Front-loading:** the Prelims Grid starts by 2:30, and the lead Deep Dive's traps land before 15:00.
>
> **Overflow cascade** (apply in order and stop at 30:00 or less):
> 1. Move `[EXT]` rows out and cap each Deep Dive at 6:30.
> 2. Cap Updates at 30 s.
> 3. Demote Deep Dive 2 to a 2:30 Focus.
> 4. Drop the Focus.
> 5. Move Mains-only items to the monthly compilation.
>
> **Never cut** the Prelims Grid below 15 items, the AP–TG Desk (until 24 Jan 2027), the pre-question, the per-pillar checkpoints, or the Trap Vault's spaced recall.

## D. Teaching and screen rules (from the reviews)

Add these to CLAUDE.md §4.2, §5.9 and §6.2:

> - **UPSC formats only.** Every on-screen question (pre-question, MCQ, Trap Vault item) uses a real UPSC format: "how many of the above", Statement-I/II, pairs, or a single-correct option. No yes/no questions.
> - **Always state the answer, with a one-line reason,** after a consistent 6–8 s hold. Never leave an on-screen question unanswered.
> - **Numbers on revision cards appear at their final value.** No count-up animations on any number a viewer might pause and copy.
> - **Fact maps, not label maps.** Each concept-map node states a testable fact ("Donbas = Donetsk + Luhansk"), not a heading ("Civilians"). The recap blanks test the week's most examinable facts.
> - **Bridge to India.** Every foreign anchor gets one India link where a real one exists. Examples from the reviews:
>   - ZNPP's VVER-1000 reactors → the same reactor family at Kudankulam;
>   - the river-bank rule → the Ganga's tributaries.
> - **Name chapters by exam hook,** for example "Montreux vs Kerch (Prelims)". Never use internal labels ("opening line", "Card: W43 Short").
> - **State the whole list when a list is the trap.** For example, UN Charter Art 33 names seven methods, not three.

## E. Process: replaces PLAYBOOK.md "Start this week's video", steps 3–6

> 3. **Triage table (mandatory, before any tier).** In `01-research-brief.md` §1, list **at least 25 candidates** from the sweep. For each, record:
>    - date, a one-line summary and a primary source;
>    - scope and GS paper;
>    - the static anchor, marked *arising* or *borrowed*;
>    - EYS with its components;
>    - the consensus check;
>    - the tracker ID.
>
>    Sort by EYS. **No Deep Dive is named until this table exists and covers the whole Mon–Sat window.** A story picked on its event day stays provisional until Wednesday's triage confirms it on score.
> 4. **Tier from the table, by rule** (CLAUDE.md §3.1). The Deep Dives are the top-scoring items that meet the India-centricity and arc-fatigue rules. The next 18–22 items fill the Prelims Grid and the AP–TG Desk.
> 5. **Continuity check** (§3.3). Score Updates on their delta.
> 6. **Propose the rundown** to Mounika with:
>    - the **sorted triage table** attached;
>    - the runtime estimate;
>    - each Deep Dive's central question;
>    - a "dropped, with EYS" list.
>
>    She approves, or swaps against the scores. A swap that promotes an item below EYS 7 to a Deep Dive is logged with a reason.
>
> **Samples and pilots follow the same triage.** A reference sample must be the top-ranked Deep Dive in a real triage table, not a story chosen for how well it shows off the craft.
