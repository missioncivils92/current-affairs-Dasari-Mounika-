# Narration that builds a following (28 Oct 2026)

**Question (owner):** how should the narration be refined so that it has "great impact on aspirants to follow my channel"?
**Applies to:** the W44 Prelims Grid and AP–TG Desk v7 scripts, and to every standalone breadth video from now on (decision 36).

## What changed in the scripts (v6 → v7)

1. **Promise in the first 15 s.** The tagline, then what this video does for you: "the one video that turns a whole week of news into the questions UPSC could actually ask you". There is no long welcome.
2. **Opening question.** The question is in UPSC format, with a 7 s countdown, and is answered later in the video.
   - In the Grid it is answered at the first trap (Yanam, Karaikal, Mahe: none are in AP).
   - In the Desk it is answered at the last trap (Kolleru, Chilika, Pulicat: only one is freshwater).
3. **One thread.** The Grid is a sixteen-station journey and the Desk follows "who holds the pen?". Every topic opens with a bridge from the last one.
4. **Viewer participation.** Viewers keep a tick score, with a halfway check and a total at the end.
5. **Mid-video promise.** The board and the halfway check both promise "the examiner's three tricks" at the end.
6. **Payoff slide.** "The examiner's three tricks" names the week's trap patterns:
   - Grid: swap the body, move the place, stretch the set.
   - Desk: who holds the pen, allows vs requires, main stream vs tributary.

   This is the light-bulb moment. It teaches the examiner's mind, which no single-topic daily video can do.
7. **Brand line.** "We find the question hiding inside the news" (CLAUDE.md §0) is said at the payoff.
8. **Close.** "One honest week at a time" plus a community line ("you do not have to do it alone"). Then **one** CTA after the payoff: subscribe, then the pinned comment (PDF, MCQ challenge, share your score).
9. **Guardrails kept:** no rank or selection promises, no hustle or fear framing, and humour aimed only at concepts. Facts and slide cue words are unchanged, and both scene files pass `--strict`.
