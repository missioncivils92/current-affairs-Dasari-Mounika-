# Selection review: why the Oleshky sample is "irrelevant for exam study" (1/1)

**Reviewer lens:** head of content at a UPSC current-affairs programme. **Date:** Tue 27 Oct 2026.
**Materials read:** CLAUDE.md §0–4, PLAYBOOK.md ("Start this week's video" and Mon–Wed), story-tracker.md, 01-research-brief.md, 02-master-script.md. Contact sheets of both cuts at 1 frame every 4 s: the hub file runs **10:00** and the Extended file runs **13:31**.

## Verdict (short version)

**The choice failed, and the rules let it through.** The execution is careful: sourced facts, honest `[VERIFY]` handling, and good look-alike traps. But about 23 minutes of finished video went to a story that would earn a **2/10** on exam yield. Four process faults caused it:

1. **The topic was picked before the week existed.** The brief is dated 27 Oct, the event day. Its coverage window is Mon 26 – Sat 31 Oct, but no triage table or sweep is in evidence. The tracker had already labelled ST-006 "W44 (Deep Dive sample)". Momentum from the W43 Short and the PLAYBOOK's own Russia–Ukraine model example primed the pick.
2. **The selection test is a floor, not a ranking.** "Pass 2 of 5" admits almost any foreign story, because anyone can attach a borrowed static anchor (a river, a convention) to it. Nothing compares candidates against each other.
3. **The escalation signals are too loose.** "A geopolitical shift involving India" was read as satisfied by any war that touches crude prices. A local humanitarian pause is not a shift, and India is not a party to it.
4. **The format rewards depth over breadth.** Three Deep Dives at 5–8 min each take about 18 min of a 28-min hub. The overflow cascade then cuts the News Grid to about 9 Glimpses before it touches depth. For Prelims, which is the channel's stated first audience, that is the wrong way round.

The real exam value of this material (the Montreux/Kerch trap, the Sea of Azov, Donbas ≠ Kherson, India and Additional Protocols I/II, Ukraine's seven neighbours) fits into **one 60–90 s Glimpse or Update**. Most of it was already taught in W43.

### 1d. Replacement: a numeric exam-yield score (EYS, 0–10)

| Component | Points | Score it as |
|---|---|---|
| **P: Prelims yield** | 0–3 | Count the distinct, testable static facts that **arise from this week's event**: a new Act or section, a scheme name or nodal ministry, a report and its publisher, a site, species or place, a body's composition, a treaty's membership. 0 = none; 1 = one; 2 = 2–3; 3 = 4 or more, or a whole look-alike set (members, neighbours, publishers). Borrowed anchors that would be teachable in any week score 0. |
| **M: Mains yield** | 0–2 | 2 = maps to a recurring Mains theme with a verified PYQ precedent (ECI and elections, federalism and water disputes, disaster management, energy security, climate finance); 1 = plausible; 0 = commentary only. |
| **I: India-centricity** | 0–2 | 2 = an Indian law, institution, court, scheme or policy is the actor or the object; 1 = a direct, measurable effect on India (prices, citizens, a treaty India is party to); 0 = foreign event, India a bystander. |
| **D: Durability** | 0–2 | 2 = still examinable in May 2027 without updating (law, judgment, scheme, report, designation, treaty step); 1 = a process with known next steps; 0 = episodic event likely to be superseded. |
| **S: State exams** | 0–1 | 1 = a genuine AP/TG link that is examinable for the Group-1 Screening (24 Jan 2027). |

**Tier thresholds:**
- **Deep Dive:** EYS ≥ 7, **and** I ≥ 1, **and** D ≥ 1, **and** P ≥ 2.
- **Focus:** EYS 5–6.
- **Glimpse:** EYS 3–4 with P ≥ 1.
- **Below 3:** drop, or a Short only.
- **Updates:** score the **delta**, not the story. An Update becomes a Deep Dive only if the delta alone scores ≥ 7.

**Oleshky:** P 1 (GC IV Art 17) + M 1 + I 0 + D 0 + S 0 = **2**, so it is an Update at most.

**What this means for weekly selection:**
- **Rank by the size of the testable set, not the drama of the event.** A Ramsar addition, a fund's first allocation, a body India joins, or a report and its publisher each give UPSC a ready "how many of the following" set. A town-level ceasefire gives none.
- **Lead with India's institutions.** ECI, SC, RBI, IMD/NDMA, MoSPI and NMC are where UPSC draws GS-2 and GS-3 Prelims facts. Foreign conflicts deserve map-set Glimpses, not Deep Dives, unless India's policy changes.
- **Breadth is the Prelims product.** A topper's weekly notes hold 20–30 items, each one static fact deep. Depth is the Mains product, and Mains is the channel's second audience.
- **Do not recycle an arc because we covered it.** W43 already taught Ukraine's borders. The marginal yield of a second Russia–Ukraine week is close to zero.

## 4. Format check: is "26–30 min with 2–3 Deep Dives of 5–8 min" the right shape?

**No, it is too depth-heavy for an audience whose first priority is Prelims.** Under the current typical-week plan (CLAUDE.md §4.1), about 18 of 29 minutes go to three Deep Dives, while the News Grid gets 2:30 (about 5–9 Glimpses). Over a year that is roughly 150 Deep Dives and 400 Glimpses. A Prelims candidate needs closer to 1,000+ testable items with anchors.

**How strong programmes are shaped (described from general knowledge; I could not verify specific runtimes in search):**
- They **split products**: breadth products (daily or weekly "news in brief", monthly compilations, a PT-365-style annual consolidation) and depth products (VisionIAS's *Weekly Focus* is described as a weekly multidimensional analysis of current themes, published separately).
- The channel's model is right to combine them in one weekly video, but the mix should flip toward breadth.

**Recommended weekly hub (30:00):**

| Segment | Minutes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open + pre-question | 0:45 | The pre-question comes from the **Prelims Grid**, not from a Deep Dive |
| Update Desk | 2:00 | 4–6 tracker deltas × 20–30 s each |
| **Prelims Grid** (by scope: Local → National → International → Misc) | **10:00–11:00** | **18–22 items × 25–35 s**. Each item: what happened → the static set → one trap. On-screen card per item, reused in the PDF |
| **Deep Issue 1** (must be India-centric, EYS ≥ 8) | 6:00–6:30 | The 5 steps, compressed. The rest goes to Extended |
| **Deep Issue 2** (EYS ≥ 7) | 5:00–5:30 | As above |
| Focus (0–1, including the Telugu States Lens) | 0–2:00 | EYS 5–6 items with a Mains angle |
| Trap Vault rapid-fire | 2:30 | 10 "how many / Statement-I/II" items from this week + spaced recall |
| Verdict + CTA | 0:45 | |

- **Front-load breadth.** The Prelims Grid starts by minute 4, because most viewers do not reach minute 25 (CLAUDE.md already concedes this).
- **Decode Extended** carries the full depth of the top Deep Issue only (0–1 a week), as now.
- **The monthly compilation** carries Mains-only themes with no deadline (e.g., Western Ghats ESA).
