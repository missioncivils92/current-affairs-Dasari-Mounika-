**Faculty judgement on the kind of international CA asked (not a verified count):**
- **Group-1 Prelims (AP and TG):** international CA is a minority strand. It is crowded out by AP/TG schemes, budget and Socio-Economic Survey numbers, and national schemes and indices. The international items that do appear are typically:
  - summits and their venues/hosts (G20, BRICS, SCO, QUAD, EAS);
  - organisation HQs, members and new members;
  - index publishers and India's rank;
  - India's bilateral agreements and exercises;
  - persons, awards and sport;
  - "places in news", always as **direct factual one-liners**.
  - Statement-type questions exist, but conflict mechanics (truce types, river-bank rules, treaty regimes over straits) are rare.
- **Group-1 Mains:**
  - **APPSC:** no paper is a dedicated IR paper [VERIFY current scheme]. International issues enter only as India's foreign-policy or economic impact inside polity, governance or economy papers.
  - **TGPSC:** the Mains weighting goes to Telangana movement and state formation, Telangana economy, society and governance, and science/technology/data interpretation. A Ukraine-ceasefire question is very unlikely. "Impact of the Russia–Ukraine war on India's food, fuel and fertiliser" is the outer bound, and only as a sub-part.
- **Group-2:** factual CA with a heavier state-and-national share. International items are one-liners (awards, summits, rankings).
- **Group-3:** state GK, schemes, society and the Telangana movement. International CA is negligible.

## 4. Direct-and-factual style: does the video suit Group-1?

| Element | Suits UPSC | Suits Group-1 Prelims | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story arc (central question, "therefore/but" beats) | ✔ | ✘ | State Prelims rewards recall of names, numbers and places, not causal chains. The arc spends ~70 % of runtime on context. |
| Satellite maps / Dnipro trace / neighbours map | ✔ | ✔ (neighbours map) | The neighbours map is exactly the state "map-based GK" style. The river-bank animation is slow for its yield. |
| Analogies (Hyderabad ambulance corridor) | ✔ (Mains) | ✘ | Adds runtime, not facts. It is the only Telugu touch and is EXT-only. |
| Trap lines ("UPSC may ask X; the trap is Y") | ✔ | ✔ | This format transfers perfectly to Group-1 Prelims. Keep it. |
| 7 s "Pause and answer" countdowns | ✔ | ~ | Good for retention, but a state viewer at 1.5× wants more items, not longer holds. |
| "Write this down" numbers card | ✔ | ✔ | The right instinct. State aspirants want this for **AP/TG numbers**. |

**What serves state aspirants better: all three formats, with different jobs.**
1. **Telugu States block in the News Grid (weekly, always).** Rapid-fire: fact → static anchor → one trap, about 25–35 s each, with a primary AP/TG document on screen (GO number, gazette, budget page). This is the main vehicle.
2. **A 60–90 s Telugu States Lens inside a Deep Dive, only when genuine.** This week, dam safety off Kakhovka. Its job is Mains depth for state papers.
3. **A state-specific Focus segment (2–3 min) when a state story is itself Deep-Dive-grade.** This week: the AP BC-quota and local-body polls ruling (constitutional, SC-level, AP-specific).
