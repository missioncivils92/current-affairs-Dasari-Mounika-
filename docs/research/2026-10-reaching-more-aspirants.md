## What we changed (v8, decision 37)
1. **The opening question comes at 0:19, not 0:53.** The order is now: tagline and promise, then the warm-up question, then the board. This protects the first 30 seconds.
2. **Spoken search words in the first 20 s.** "UPSC current affairs from the twenty-sixth of October to the first of November, for Prelims 2027 and Group-1" in the Grid, and "AP and Telangana current affairs for APPSC and TGPSC Group-1" in the Desk. Viewers know at once they are in the right place, and the words are in the captions.
3. **Reach CTA, given once after the payoff:** subscribe, **send it to one friend who is preparing with you**, and share your score under the pinned comment. Sharing between study partners is the cheapest reach there is.
4. **Packaging pack** (`weeks/2026-W44/packaging/`):
   - three titles and three rendered thumbnails per video, for Test & Compare;
   - descriptions with question-style chapters;
   - pinned MCQ challenges;
   - end screen and cards linking the two videos to each other;
   - Community and Telegram posts.

   The thumbnails follow §7: at most 3 words, top-left, a hero visual on the right, the brand bottom-left, no face, and Survey-of-India maps.
5. **Shorts pack** (`weeks/2026-W44/04-shorts-pack.md`): five 45–60 s Shorts, Mon–Fri. Each uses YouTube's "Related video" link to send viewers to the long video.
6. **Proposed, owner's call:** a Telugu audio track for the Desk recorded by Mounika herself (never AI dubbing, §1), or a human-checked Telugu subtitle file.
