## Q1. TGPSC Group-1: pattern, papers, medium, the 2024 cycle, the litigation and new notifications

### Takeaway
TGPSC Group-1 (Notification 02/2024, dated 19 Feb 2024, 563 posts) has a 150-question objective Prelims (GS plus mental ability) and a Mains of seven papers. General English is qualifying at Class X level. Papers I–VI carry 150 marks each, take three hours each, and can be written in English, Telugu or Urdu. There is **no interview**. The 2024 cycle survived a single-judge order quashing the results (9 Sep 2025). A Division Bench restored the results (5 Feb 2026), and the Supreme Court declined to interfere (around 2 Apr 2026). As of October 2026, **no new TGPSC Group-1 notification has been found**.

### Cited Findings
**Pattern**

General English must be answered in English; the other papers in English, Telugu or Urdu. No negative marking in Mains. — [Deccan Chronicle: TGPSC announces Group-I Mains schedule](https://deccanchronicle.com/education/tgpsc-announces-group-i-mains-exam-schedule-898632); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/tgpsc-group-1-prelims-results-released-mains-exam-date-announced-3057419/amp/)
- Mains papers as listed by coaching aggregators: I General Essay; II History, Culture and Geography; III Indian Society, Constitution and Governance; IV Economy and Development; V Science & Technology and Data Interpretation; VI Telangana Movement and State Formation. ## Q2. APPSC Group-1: pattern, papers, the 12/2023 cycle and the 2026 notification

### Takeaway
APPSC Group-1 Prelims (screening) is two objective papers of 120 marks each with one-third negative marking. Mains has two qualifying language papers (Telugu, English) plus five merit papers of 150 marks each (750), followed by a 75-mark interview. Notification 12/2023 (8 Dec 2023, 81 posts advertised) ran Prelims on 17 Mar 2024 and Mains in early May 2025, with a final list of 87 selected around 30 Jan 2026. A **new Notification 07/2026** (166 posts per the detailed notification; some sites say 163) was issued with applications open to **27 Oct 2026** and **Prelims on 24 Jan 2027**.

### Cited Findings
**Pattern**
- Prelims: Paper I GS (History & Culture, Polity, Economy & Planning, Geography; 4 × 30 questions) and Paper II (Mental Ability, Science & Technology, Current Events; 3 × 40 questions, as reported, or 30 each depending on source); 120 minutes each; 240 marks total. — [Adda247 APPSC Group 1 exam analysis](https://www.adda247.com/upsc-exam/appsc-group-1-exam-analysis/); [Adda247 question paper](https://www.adda247.com/upsc-exam/appsc-group-1-question-paper/) **[VERIFY Paper II section split]**
- Negative marking of one-third per wrong answer; Prelims is a screening test only, and its marks do not count toward final merit. — [Carpe Diem IAS PYQs](https://carpediemias.com/nw/appsc-group-1-prelims-previous-year-question-papers); [Adda247 syllabus](https://www.adda247.com/exams/andhra-pradesh/appsc-group-1-syllabus/)
- Mains: seven papers of 150 marks each. Telugu and English are qualifying. The five merit papers are Paper I General Essay; II History & Culture and Geography of India and AP; III Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law & Ethics; IV Economy & Development of India and AP; V Science & Technology and Environmental Issues. Interview is 75 marks. — [Adda247 APPSC syllabus 2026](https://www.adda247.com/exams/andhra-pradesh/appsc-group-1-syllabus/); [CareerPower syllabus](https://www.careerpower.in/appsc-group-1-syllabus.html); [KP IAS 2026 breakdown](https://kpiasacademy.com/appsc-group-1-syllabus-2026/). ## Q3. How current affairs and state-specific content appear (with example questions)

### Takeaway
Both commissions test current affairs explicitly. In TGPSC Prelims this is the GS syllabus line "Current Affairs – Regional, National & International". In APPSC it is "Current Events of national and international importance" in Prelims Paper II. Mains papers embed state development content: Telangana movement history in TGPSC Paper VI, and AP economy and Reorganisation issues in APPSC Papers II and IV. I could not retrieve verbatim exam questions in this session.

### Cited Findings
- TGPSC Prelims syllabus includes "Current Affairs – Regional, National & International", along with Telangana art and literature. — [StudyIQ](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/tspsc-group-1-question-paper/)
- The TGPSC Paper VI model paper (coaching, 31 Aug 2024; not the actual exam) covers: the Telangana song movement (Dhoom-Dham), the Srikrishna Committee, AP Reorganisation Act 2014, the Warangal Declaration (1997), the Million March (2011) and the Girglani Committee (on GO 610 violations). It has three sections of five questions with either/or choices. — [Sarma Potayya blog model paper](https://sarmapotayya.blogspot.com/2024/09/tspsc-group-i-mains2024-group-i-mains.html)
- Actual 2024 Mains Paper II and III question PDFs with analysis are hosted by KP IAS. They were not opened (fetch blocked). — [KP IAS Paper III analysis (24 Oct 2024)](https://kpiasacademy.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/TGPSC-GROUP-1-Mains-PAPER-III-Dt.24.10.24_Analysis.pdf); [KP IAS Paper II 2024](https://kpiasacademy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TGPSC-Grp-1-Mains-Paper-II-2024.pdf); [KP IAS PYQ page](https://kpiasacademy.com/tgpsc-group-1-previous-year-question-papers/)
- Political sensitivity: a December 2024 TGPSC **Group-II** paper drew criticism for an alleged focus on NTR/Naidu/TDP over the statehood movement. This shows how politically scrutinised state-history questions are. — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/tgpsc-group-ii-paper-kicks-up-storm-with-its-political-bias-and-focus-on-naidu-tdp)
- APPSC Prelims Paper II carries a full "Current Events" section of about 30–40 questions. — [Adda247 exam analysis](https://www.adda247.com/upsc-exam/appsc-group-1-exam-analysis/)

### Inferences
- Recurrent state-anchor topics that fit both syllabi: AP Reorganisation Act 2014 (Schedules IX/X, Section 94 special assistance, Polavaram as a national project under Section 90), the Krishna Water Disputes Tribunal-II and KRMB/GRMB jurisdiction, Telangana movement milestones (1952 Mulki agitation, 1969 agitation, 1997 Warangal Declaration, 2009 KCR fast and 9 Dec announcement, 2010 Srikrishna Committee, 2011 Sakala Janula Samme and Million March, 2 June 2014 formation), state budgets and Socio-Economic Surveys, and flagship schemes. These are inferred from the syllabus wording and the model paper, not from verified PYQs.
- Style inference: Group-1 Prelims leans more factual and direct ("Which committee…", "In which year…") than UPSC's multi-statement format. It includes Mental Ability/Data Interpretation (TGPSC) and Aptitude (APPSC). TGPSC Mains Paper V explicitly includes Data Interpretation. Group-1 Mains questions are more often "explain / describe" (descriptive) than UPSC's "critically examine". These are coaching-analysis impressions; no quantified source was found.

- **Verbatim example questions with year were not retrievable** because question-paper PDFs could not be fetched. The report writer should not present any specific question as a PYQ until it has been pulled from the KP IAS, TGNNS or APPSC PDFs.
- No source quantified the current-affairs share (as a percentage of questions) in either Prelims.

## Q4. Question style vs UPSC; language trends; cross-exam reuse

### Takeaway
Both state exams are reported as "easy to moderate" against UPSC's "difficult", with more direct factual questions plus a heavy state component. Telugu-medium writers were about 39% in TGPSC Mains 2024. Current-affairs reuse is strongest for RBI Grade B ESI (government schemes and economy) and the AP/TS Group-2/3 and SI exams. Details for the other exams are largely unverified in this session.

### Cited Findings
- TGPSC Prelims 2024 was rated easy to moderate. — [StudyIQ](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/tspsc-group-1-question-paper/). APPSC Prelims 2024 was rated easy to moderate. — [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/upsc-exam/appsc-group-1-exam-analysis/)
- TGPSC Mains 2024 medium split: English 12,323, Telugu 7,829, Urdu 9. — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-high-court-strikes-down-group-1-mains-results-calls-for-reevaluation)
- RBI Grade B: per a coaching source, about 40% of Phase-1 GA and about 70% of Phase-2 ESI/FM questions were directly or indirectly current-affairs based. Government schemes and ESI/finance current affairs are frequent topics. — [EduTap](https://edutap.in/rbi-grade-b/preparation/current-affairs-for-rbi-grade-b/); [EduTap ESI syllabus](https://edutap.in/rbi-grade-b/syllabus/what-is-the-economic-and-social-issues-syllabus-of-rbi-grade-b/)
