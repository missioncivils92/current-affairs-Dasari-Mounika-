# PLAYBOOK.md — Weekly Production Checklist

Each week runs from Monday triage to the Sunday 6:00 PM IST upload. Rules, tone and formats are defined in `CLAUDE.md`. This file covers **what to do, when, and who does it**.

**Owners:** **C** = Claude (research, scripts, packaging) · **M** = Mounika (review, voiceover recording; voice-only, never on camera) · **E** = editor (visuals, cut, upload)

**Week folder:** `weeks/YYYY-Www/`. Create it on Monday by copying the five weekly templates from `templates/`.

---

## Weekly timeline at a glance

| Day | Main output | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Week folder + rolling story log | Short #1 (from last week's leftover trap) |
| Tue | Log continues; pillar shortlist forming | Short #2 |
| Wed | **Pillars locked**; research brief (5-step) started | Short #3 |
| Thu | Research brief complete + verification log | Short #4 (optional) |
| Fri | **Master script v1** + visual handoff | Short #5 (optional) |
| Sat | M review, QC, record voiceover; PDF notes + MCQs | — |
| Sun | Edit lock by 2 PM, packaging, **upload 6:00 PM IST** | — |

---

## Monday: set up and triage

- [ ] **C** Create `weeks/YYYY-Www/` and copy `templates/01`–`05` into it. Fill in the week's Mon–Sat dates.
- [ ] **C** Start the **rolling story log** in `01-research-brief.md` §1. For each candidate story, add a one-line summary, source link, GS mapping and selection-test score (see `CLAUDE.md` §3).
- [ ] **C** Sources to scan daily: The Hindu, Indian Express, PIB (all ministries), LiveMint, Down To Earth, PRS, the SC daily judgments list, RBI press releases, and AP/Telangana government press releases.
- [ ] **E/M** Publish Short #1, built from a trap left over from last week's pack.

## Tuesday: keep logging, build the shortlist

- [ ] **C** Keep adding to the story log. Merge related stories into **themes**: for example, three separate stories about rare earths, a China export curb and a new KABIL deal become one "Critical Minerals" theme.
- [ ] **C** Flag likely Telugu-state links.
- [ ] **E/M** Publish Short #2.

## Wednesday: lock the pillars

- [ ] **C** Propose **4–5 pillars**, each with a one-line pitch, GS paper(s), static anchor and estimated runtime. Together they must cover at least 3 GS papers.
- [ ] **M** Approve or swap pillars. **Decision deadline: Wednesday 8 PM.** If a big story breaks after this, see "Late-breaking news" below.
- [ ] **C** Start the 5-step analysis for each pillar in `01-research-brief.md` §3.
- [ ] **E/M** Publish Short #3.

## Thursday: finish the research brief

- [ ] **C** Complete all 5 steps for every pillar, including at least 3 Prelims traps and 4 Mains dimensions with data, plus the committee-backed Way Forward, the model Mains question and the answer skeleton.
- [ ] **C** Add the Telugu States Lens where it is genuine.
- [ ] **C** Fill in the **verification log**. Every number, date, Article, ministry and index needs a primary-source link. Clear every `[VERIFY]` tag or cut the claim.
- [ ] **C** Draft 15 MCQ candidates (of which 10 make the PDF) and 3 Mains questions (of which 2 make the PDF).
- [ ] **E/M** Publish Short #4 (optional).

## Friday: script and visual handoff

- [ ] **C** Write the **master script v1** in `02-master-script.md`. It must be verbatim, carry tags on every block, and show a running word count. Budget: target minutes × 135 words.
  - Suggested runtime split for 22 min:
    - Cold open: 0:45
    - Pillars: 4 × 4:00
    - Trap Vault rapid-fire recap: 1:30
    - Telugu States Lens: 1:00–2:00, split across pillars or as one block
    - Verdict + CTA: 1:15
- [ ] **C** Fill in the **visual handoff** in `03-visual-handoff.md`: a shot-by-shot table with timestamps, every hyperframe prompt, kinetic graphic and map, plus an asset checklist.
- [ ] **C** Draft 3 title + thumbnail concepts and the description with chapters.
- [ ] **C** Fill in the **shorts pack** in `04-shorts-pack.md` for next week's Mon–Fri slots (5 scripts).
- [ ] **E** Start generating hyperframes and building motion graphics from the handoff.
- [ ] **E/M** Publish Short #5 (optional).

## Saturday: review, QC, record

- [ ] **M** Read the script aloud once. Mark any line that doesn't sound natural. **C** revises it to v2.
- [ ] **C/M** Run the **QC checklist** before recording:
  - [ ] Every word to be spoken is written in full. No bullet points inside narration.
  - [ ] Runtime is 18–26 min (word count ÷ 135).
  - [ ] No "Welcome back" opener. The cold open is inside the event.
  - [ ] Every pillar has all 5 steps, at least 3 traps, 4 Mains dimensions, a cited committee or report, a model question and an answer skeleton.
  - [ ] At least 3 GS papers covered across the pillars.
  - [ ] Every block has a visual tag. Every map tag notes "official Survey of India boundaries".
  - [ ] No on-camera blocks. Direct-address lines use `[VOICE ANCHOR]` with a voice direction. No faces, hands or AI avatars standing in for Mounika.
  - [ ] Phonetic markers on first use. New terms added to the `CLAUDE.md` §8 bank.
  - [ ] Verification log complete. Zero `[VERIFY]` tags remain.
  - [ ] Political claims are attributed. No partisan adjectives.
  - [ ] Exactly one CTA.
- [ ] **M** Record the voiceover (VO), one WAV file per segment: `YYYY-Www_VO_open.wav`, `YYYY-Www_VO_P1.wav`, and so on. Use the same mic, room and distance every week so the voice sounds identical.
- [ ] **C** Produce the **PDF notes + MCQs** in `05-weekly-notes-mcq.md`:
  - One page per pillar (anchor, traps, 4 dimensions, Way Forward, Telugu lens).
  - 10 MCQs in UPSC format: multi-statement "How many of the above", assertion–reason, pairs. The answer key and explanations go at the end.
  - 2 Mains questions with skeletons.
  - Export to PDF with the channel branding.
- [ ] **E** Rough cut.

## Sunday: lock and publish

- [ ] **E** Final cut and edit lock by **2:00 PM IST**. **M** watches it through once.
- [ ] **M** Pick the final title and thumbnail from the 3 concepts. **E** designs the thumbnail (at most 3 words, top-left, white and yellow; hero object on the right third; "DECODE UPSC" badge; no face).
- [ ] **E** Upload as scheduled for **6:00 PM IST**. Include the description, chapters, sources, PDF link, end screen (last week's video + playlist) and cards.
- [ ] **E** Pinned comment: "This week's MCQ challenge" (1 question; answer revealed in Monday's Short #1) + PDF link.
- [ ] **M** Spend 30–60 minutes replying to comments after publishing.
- [ ] **C** Write next week's carry-forward list in `weeks/YYYY-Www/README.md`: stories still developing, unanswered viewer questions, and traps not used.

## Monthly compilation (last Sunday of the month, or the first weekend of the next month)

- [ ] **C** On the Wednesday before, merge the month's four research briefs into `months/YYYY-MM/` using `templates/06-monthly-compilation.md`.
  - Group by GS paper, not by week.
  - Update developing stories: a bill passed, a judgment delivered, a treaty ratified.
  - Rebuild the Trap Vault into a 30-question rapid-fire round.
  - Add a "Telugu States Lens" monthly roundup for Group-1 viewers.
- [ ] **C** Write new linking narration (verbatim). Reuse the weekly VO only where the facts haven't changed.
- [ ] **M** Record the new segments. **E** assembles a 60–90 min video with chapters per GS paper.
- [ ] **C** Produce a combined monthly PDF with 30 MCQs and 5 Mains questions.

## Late-breaking news (after the Wednesday lock)

1. If it scores at least 3/4 on the selection test **and** happens before Friday noon: swap out the weakest pillar.
2. If it happens after Friday noon: cover it as a 60–90 s "Breaking Lens" segment before the verdict, or carry it forward to next week.
3. Never ship a breaking item without completing the verification log.

## Metrics review (every Monday, 15 min, **M + C**)

Look at last week's video:
- CTR on the chosen thumbnail.
- Average view duration and the retention dips by chapter.
- Shorts → long-form click-through.
- PDF downloads.
- Top comments and questions.

Log one change to try this week in `weeks/YYYY-Www/README.md`.
